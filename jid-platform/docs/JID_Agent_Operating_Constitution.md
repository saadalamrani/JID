# جِد | JID — TRUST & RIGHTS CONSTITUTION
## دستور جِد للثقة والحقوق

**Adopted:** 2026-08-26 (Asia/Riyadh)
**Status:** Founder-adopted constitutional authority
**Supersedes:** the previous product-heavy `JID_Agent_Operating_Constitution.md`

This Constitution is intentionally small and durable. It governs rights, trust, truth,
privacy, security, fairness, accountability, and commercial integrity. Product models,
actors, waves, features, pricing, vendors, technical stack choices, roadmaps, and design
rules belong in dated operating doctrine and approved task packets, not in constitutional
articles.

Applicable law and lawful regulatory obligations always outrank this Constitution.

---

## Article 1 — Human dignity and agency

People are not inventory. JID must not exploit career anxiety, coerce unnecessary
disclosure, fabricate urgency, or design a consequential process that removes meaningful
human agency without a lawful and explicitly approved basis.

## Article 2 — Truth and traceability

No metric, percentage, badge, verification, recommendation, outcome, institutional claim,
AI claim, or marketing statement may be presented without a real source, a defined
meaning, an appropriate method, and limitations proportionate to the claim.

Unknown, missing, inferred, self-declared, externally sourced, verified, disputed, stale,
and revoked states must not be silently collapsed into one another.

## Article 3 — Purpose limitation and data minimization

JID collects, uses, retains, shares, and derives personal data only for defined and
lawfully supported purposes, using the minimum data reasonably necessary for those
purposes.

Personal data is not a saleable product. JID shall not sell, barter, or covertly repurpose
personal data as a revenue stream.

## Article 4 — User rights and control

JID must provide usable mechanisms, as applicable under law and the relevant processing
basis, for notice, access, correction, export, withdrawal or objection, deletion or
retention handling, and challenge of material errors.

Material disclosures and consequential uses of personal evidence must be attributable and
reviewable. Rights and essential privacy controls are not premium features.

## Article 5 — Security and privacy by design

Authorization must be enforced before protected data reaches an unauthorized recipient or
client. Client-side hiding is never a privacy boundary.

JID must apply least privilege, auditable privileged access, appropriate tenant and actor
isolation, secure handling of secrets, controlled production access, incident response,
and defense in depth proportionate to risk.

## Article 6 — Fairness, accessibility, and non-discrimination

Employment and career processes must use criteria relevant to their legitimate purpose and
must not knowingly introduce unlawful discrimination or inaccessible barriers.

JID must support reasonable accessibility, understandable interaction, and accommodation
paths where consequential workflows require them. Fairness claims require evidence; the
absence of complaints is not proof of fairness.

## Article 7 — Human accountability for consequential decisions

AI may assist, retrieve, summarize, compare, draft, explain, recommend, and prepare within
approved authority. Consequential employment decisions remain attributable to accountable
humans unless a future lawful, validated, explicitly adopted governance decision states
otherwise.

JID shall not use facial, voice, or emotion inference, fabricated fit or employability
percentages, or opaque automated personality/culture-fit scoring as a basis for adverse
employment decisions.

## Article 8 — Institutional and commercial integrity

JID shall not imply government endorsement, official statistical authority, legal
compliance, certification, partnership status, employment outcomes, or institutional
performance without evidence appropriate to the claim.

JID shall not use pay-to-win organic ranking, job guarantees, fabricated scarcity, dark
pricing, or commercial terms that weaken baseline rights, privacy, security, or truthful
relevance.

## Article 9 — Accountability, reversibility, and stop authority

Material high-risk capabilities require a named owner, an audit path, an incident route,
and a practical pause, kill, rollback, or containment mechanism where technically and
operationally applicable.

Credible evidence of material harm, security compromise, unlawful processing, invalid
assessment use, or materially misleading claims is sufficient to pause a capability while
it is reviewed. Negative evidence must be preserved rather than hidden.

---

## Governance and amendment

This Constitution may not be amended silently. Every amendment must record:

- the article being changed;
- old and new text;
- reason and evidence;
- affected rights and actors;
- legal/privacy/security review where relevant;
- founder approval; and
- effective date.

Emergency security, privacy, legal, or safety action may pause a capability immediately,
but emergency action cannot permanently amend this Constitution.

## What is not constitutional

The following are governed elsewhere and may change through dated founder decisions and
approved operating packets provided they remain consistent with this Constitution:

- product definition and actor model;
- Career Record, Opportunity Graph, Radar, Lammah, Abhathli, social/professional, hiring,
  assessment, University, and Government product decisions;
- wave sequence and delivery model;
- pricing and packaging;
- geography and expansion strategy;
- vendor, OSS, model, database, framework, design-system, and infrastructure choices;
- research findings, roadmaps, KPIs, task packets, and launch plans.

The active operating doctrine is maintained separately under
`docs/command-center/WAVE_OPERATING_MODEL.md`.
