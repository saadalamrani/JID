# JID UX/CX Strategy Report 2026

**Role:** UX/CX Director — official representative of the Individual sector
**Study date:** 2026-08-25
**Status:** Independent Individual-experience recommendation; not adopted; no implementation authority
**Evidence labels:** `FACT`, `RESEARCH FINDING`, `MARKET SIGNAL`, `HYPOTHESIS`, `OPINION`, `RECOMMENDATION`

## 1. Executive Summary

JID should feel like a calm, living career workspace—not a profile form, static dashboard, application counter, or public performance stage.

**[RECOMMENDATION]** The Individual experience should center on four verbs: **Know, Discover, Act, Grow**. Career Record supports Know; Opportunity Graph supports Discover; Radar and the bounded employer loop support Act; evidence updates, mentors, preparation, and purposeful knowledge support Grow.

The first 90-day journey is mobile-first and Arabic-first: create/import a minimum useful Career Record; immediately receive a truthful output; explore source-labeled opportunities; see reasons, gaps, constraints, deadline, and freshness; save or act; track the next step in Radar; receive employer closure where available; update evidence. Abhathli may explain, monitor, draft, and rehearse, but never mass auto-apply, invent experience, or take irreversible external action silently.

**[RECOMMENDATION]** Reopen professional interaction through a **Purposeful Professional Layer**: profile-bound projects and verified achievements, mentor articles, topic collections, and finite opportunity/event/cohort conversations. Do not launch an infinite engagement-ranked feed, follower contest, or paid organic reach.

**[RECOMMENDATION]** Choose hybrid rebuild so tested identity/privacy/i18n foundations survive while the information architecture, interaction model, and fragmented career truth are rebuilt progressively. [S01–S04]

## 2. Mandate

UX/CX represents the Individual across student, graduate, active job seeker, employee, career changer, returner, mentor, and senior professional contexts. The mandate includes research, end-to-end journey, emotional load, accessibility, Arabic-first/LTR parity, service recovery, privacy comprehension, repeated utility, and willingness to pay.

The role must challenge any design that extracts Individual data primarily for employer/institutional benefit, even if technically efficient.

## 3. What I Learned

**[FACT]** Current JID has Individual, opportunity, Lammah, Profile, notification, and other surfaces, but the studied repository evidence does not establish one coherent repeated journey; prior release evidence recorded partial completion and route/i18n defects. [S01–S04]

**[RESEARCH FINDING]** LinkedIn’s feed uses hundreds of profile, network, content, and activity signals and carries sponsored/recommended content. This explains why a professional network can become an attention environment; it does not prove users universally dislike it. [S09–S10]

**[RESEARCH FINDING]** WCAG 2.2 adds or emphasizes visible/unobscured focus, target size, consistent help, redundant-entry reduction, and accessible authentication—directly relevant to JID’s high-stress mobile journeys. [S13–S14]

**[RESEARCH FINDING]** ISO 9241-210 treats human-centered design as lifecycle activity, not a late usability review. [S15]

**[RESEARCH FINDING]** The ai-job-search reference shows that search, dedupe, truthful tailoring, interview preparation, follow-up drafts, and outcome tracking can form one useful personal workflow. It also warns that job pages are untrusted input and personal files need strong boundaries. [S16–S18]

## 4. Saudi Current State

Individuals in Saudi Arabia can use Jadarat as the official national employment platform, direct employer sites, LinkedIn, informal networks, and other channels. [S05] JID must therefore reduce multi-channel cognitive and operational cost rather than pretend to own every application.

Saudi-native experience requirements include:

- Arabic as an authored interaction language, not a translated shell;
- correct RTL/LTR switching and Latin digits;
- bilingual titles/skills and mixed-language evidence;
- Saudi cities, regions, sectors, graduate programs, internships, and training contexts;
- transparent distinction between native JID action and an external handoff;
- privacy rights and purpose explanations that are understandable at the moment of disclosure. [S08]

**[MARKET SIGNAL]** Founder and attached research describe distrust, performance pressure, application silence, and static product feel. These themes require user observation and behavioral validation across Saudi segments. [S19–S21]

## 5. Global Benchmark

- **LinkedIn:** powerful professional identity/distribution, but its official feed design illustrates the engagement and sponsored-reach model JID need not copy. [S09–S10]
- **Handshake:** shows mobile delivery of jobs, events, employers, and institutional career experiences; JID can learn from actor connectivity while avoiding unverified vendor claims. [S11–S12]
- **HESA/NACE:** show how outcome interfaces must expose coverage and definitions rather than present a psychologically reassuring but false percentage. [S22–S23]
- **WCAG/ISO:** accessibility and human-centered process are product quality, not a compliance footer. [S13–S15]
- **ai-job-search:** shows the value of an agent that drafts and proposes while the person remains the decision-maker. [S16–S18]

## 6. User/Customer Jobs-to-be-Done

### Know

- Understand what I can credibly claim.
- Keep evidence and history without rebuilding a CV each time.
- See what is self-declared, verified, observed, missing, or private.

### Discover

- Explore sectors, organizations, paths, and opportunities.
- Understand source, freshness, eligibility, constraints, and deadline.
- Receive explanations, not unexplained rankings.

### Act

- Save, compare, prepare, apply/depart to an external source, follow up, and track.
- Know what happened and what I can do next.

### Grow

- Update evidence, learn from outcomes, prepare for interviews/assessments, use mentors, and share structured professional knowledge.

## 7. Pain Points

- Long onboarding before any return value.
- Duplicate entry across Profile, CV, Timeline, and applications.
- Empty dashboards and generic completion nudges.
- Opportunity cards without trustworthy source/freshness or clear external handoff.
- Match scores that imply precision without explanation.
- Application silence and status ambiguity.
- Privacy toggles detached from real audience/purpose consequences.
- Content environments that provoke comparison, performance, noise, and compulsive checking.
- Arabic layouts that are technically RTL but interactionally translated.

## 8. Unmet Needs

- **Immediate utility per fact:** every contribution creates a visible benefit.
- **Progressive commitment:** start with minimum useful record, enrich when a task requires it.
- **Career continuity:** value between active searches.
- **Closure:** distinguish no response, awaiting employer, external unknown, rejected, withdrawn, expired, and completed.
- **Calm guidance:** finite next actions, not alerts designed to maximize return.
- **Purposeful interaction:** knowledge and help connected to a career context.
- **Agency:** AI proposes; the person chooses, edits, and sends.

## 9. Market Structure

The Individual assembles a career journey across government systems, job boards, professional networks, direct employer sites, messaging, documents, and human guidance. JID’s experience opportunity is orchestration and truth, not forcing every transaction to become native.

**[HYPOTHESIS]** A high-trust personal operating layer can earn repeat use even before JID owns large native opportunity supply, if it reduces re-entry, search waste, uncertainty, and missed next actions.

## 10. Competitors/Substitutes

- Jadarat for Saudi national discovery/application. [S05]
- LinkedIn for identity, network, content, jobs, and recruiter exposure. [S09–S10]
- Handshake for early-career discovery and institution-connected experience. [S11–S12]
- Direct employer sites and email for authoritative action/status.
- CV/LLM tools and ai-job-search-style agents for preparation. [S16]
- Notes, spreadsheets, calendars, and personal inboxes for tracking.
- Career centers, mentors, friends, recruiters, and professional communities for interpretation and support.

## 11. Opportunities

1. **Minimum Useful Record:** first session produces a truthful CV/profile artifact or clearer opportunity set.
2. **Opportunity Detail as trust page:** source, organization identity, freshness, deadline, eligibility, requirements, external/native action, and report control.
3. **Radar as calm state machine:** next action and last verified status, not an anxiety scoreboard.
4. **Abhathli as copilot:** monitor, explain, compare, draft, rehearse, and propose updates.
5. **Career Home as finite agenda:** relevant changes and next actions, not cards for every feature.
6. **Purposeful Professional Layer:** evidence, mentor knowledge, and contextual conversations.

## 12. Risks

- The Career Record becomes unpaid data labor for employers/universities.
- Progressive disclosure hides material privacy consequences.
- Too many notifications create anxiety and disengagement.
- External opportunities appear endorsed or current when they are not.
- AI makes generic applications faster, worsening employer trust and user disappointment.
- Professional publishing reproduces comparison and visibility inequality.
- Accessibility is checked per component rather than across complete tasks.
- Arabic and English parity is cosmetic rather than semantic.

## 13. What JID Should Do

**[RECOMMENDATION]** Design the Individual home around:

1. **One next action** with a clear reason.
2. **Opportunity changes** relevant to an explicit goal, each with source/freshness.
3. **Radar changes** and closure.
4. **Record utility**: a fact to add only when it unlocks a named benefit.
5. **Guidance** from mentors/topics related to the person’s current task.

Use finite lists, explicit “why this,” honest unknown states, editable AI drafts, and undo/revocation where possible.

## 14. What JID Should Not Do

- Do not show a general infinite feed, public popularity scoreboard, or engagement streak.
- Do not force 100% profile completion.
- Do not duplicate facts across outputs.
- Do not call an external handoff a completed application.
- Do not fabricate status, fit, salary, or employer response.
- Do not auto-apply, auto-send, or alter the canonical record from generated text.
- Do not expose private evidence to make discovery “work better” without an explicit grant.

## 15. Product/Service Implications

### Individual information architecture

- **Home / اليوم:** finite agenda and meaningful changes.
- **Record / سجلي:** facts, evidence, privacy, outputs.
- **Opportunities / الفرص:** explore, search, compare, source truth.
- **Radar / الرادار:** actions, states, follow-up, outcomes.
- **Guidance / الإرشاد:** Abhathli, mentors, preparation, structured knowledge.

### Emotional model

- Calm, specific, and non-judgmental.
- “Unknown” is a legitimate state.
- Rejection and silence are handled with closure and options, not motivational copy.
- Progress means a completed useful step, not profile percentage.
- Motion communicates state change and respects reduced-motion preferences.

## 16. Data Implications

- Store each fact once and project it.
- Show source/provenance in human language.
- Separate self-declared, verified, observed, inferred, and generated information.
- Keep Evidence private by default and audience grants granular.
- Make AI drafts ephemeral until accepted; preserve edit history for consequential actions.
- Radar distinguishes user-declared, employer-confirmed, and inferred email/status signals.
- Product analytics use real events and cohorts; no “engagement score.”

## 17. Technology Implications

Hybrid rebuild should preserve secure identity/privacy foundations and replace the experience through an adapter/projection layer. [S01–S04]

- Mobile-first rendering and full keyboard/screen-reader paths.
- Arabic-first search, mixed-language input, and bidirectional text handling.
- Offline/slow-network resilience for drafts and transparent retry.
- Source/freshness and native/external action components reused everywhere.
- Abhathli uses bounded tools and preview/confirm patterns; job content remains untrusted. [S18]
- No AI output becomes canonical or externally sent without explicit confirmation.

## 18. Privacy/Legal Implications

PDPL rights and purpose limits must become understandable interaction states, not policy links. [S08]

- Explain audience and purpose at the disclosure moment.
- Allow withdrawal/revocation and explain downstream limits.
- Separate discovery personalization from employer visibility and university outcomes consent.
- Avoid coercive consent tied to basic service when not necessary.
- Provide correction, export/access, and deletion-request journeys.
- Conduct privacy and legal review for automated processing, sensitive assessments, and cross-border AI vendors.

## 19. Business Model

Individual trust requires a strong free core: Record, Profile/CV projection, core Opportunity Graph/Radar, privacy, and truthful status. Paid utility can be tested for advanced Abhathli monitoring/preparation, premium document variants, and mentor services. The UX red line is that money cannot buy truth, employer access to private facts, or preferential factual visibility.

Employers and institutions should carry the primary early revenue burden because they receive workflow and decision value.

## 20. Partnerships

- Individuals across life stages as continuous design participants, including people with disabilities.
- Employers who agree to response/closure standards.
- Authorized opportunity sources with reliable expiry and handoff semantics.
- Career centers and mentors for guidance research.
- Assessment vendors only when candidate experience, accessibility, accommodations, validity, and appeals are supportable.

## 21. Validation Needed

### Research plan

- Contextual interviews/task observation across students, graduates, job seekers, employed explorers, career changers, and mentors.
- Arabic-first and English parity usability.
- Screen-reader, keyboard, zoom/reflow, touch, cognitive-load, and low-bandwidth testing.
- Longitudinal diary/pilot: why does someone return after 2, 4, and 8 weeks?
- Privacy comprehension: can people predict who sees what and revoke it?
- AI trust: can users identify suggestions, sources, gaps, and external actions?

### Evidence gates

- Minimum Useful Record completion and usefulness.
- repeat record reuse, not completion percentage.
- reduced redundant entry.
- opportunity source/freshness comprehension.
- Radar next-action completion and employer closure.
- notification opt-out and anxiety signals.
- accessibility of complete journeys, not isolated screens.

## 22. 90-Day Actions

### Days 0–30

- Interview and observe 25–30 Individuals across segments; numbers remain planning targets until recruited.
- Prototype Arabic-first mobile Home, Minimum Useful Record, opportunity detail, and Radar.
- Test navigation labels, privacy explanations, empty/unknown states, and source/freshness cues.
- establish accessibility acceptance for full tasks using WCAG 2.2. [S13–S14]

### Days 31–60

- Build flagged thin slice: Record → truthful CV output → Opportunity Graph → Radar → employer response.
- Add Abhathli explanation/draft/preparation preview with source and confirm controls.
- instrument task and trust events with explicit denominators.

### Days 61–90

- Run real consenting journeys and longitudinal check-ins.
- Verify Arabic/English, mobile/desktop, accessibility, slow/error, and privacy paths.
- Stop any capability that increases application volume without usefulness/closure.

## 23. 12-Month Roadmap

- **Q1:** stabilize core journey and accessibility.
- **Q2:** expand source coverage, compare/alerts, employer communication and closure.
- **Q3:** pilot mentor identity/services and finite knowledge; pilot university outcome collection only with consent/coverage.
- **Q4:** personalize next actions from observed user goals/outcomes, not opaque scores; test paid recurring utility.

## 24. 3-Year Direction

JID becomes a trusted companion across education-to-work, job change, growth, mentoring, and professional contribution. The system should be useful without public posting and richer when a person opts into purposeful contribution. Global expansion preserves the agency/evidence model while adapting language, law, opportunity sources, and career institutions.

## 25. Contribution to Saudi Leadership

JID can lead through genuinely Arabic-first career interaction, inclusive mobile access, local opportunity/program semantics, clearer source truth, lower repeat-entry burden, and respectful employer closure. This complements official Saudi employment infrastructure rather than competing on authority. [S05–S07]

## 26. Contribution to Global Potential

The globally relevant UX model is a professional system optimized for useful transitions—not engagement. A Career Record that returns immediate value, a truthful Opportunity Graph, Radar closure, user-controlled AI, and finite professional knowledge would be meaningfully different from dominant feed-centric patterns.

## 27. Disagreements with Other Strategy Office Roles

- **CEO:** a compelling OS narrative does not guarantee daily utility; do not make the Individual populate infrastructure for a future network.
- **CPO:** portfolio coherence must not compress distinct life stages into one “job seeker” flow; progressive pathways are required.
- **Business/CFO:** employer payment cannot justify opaque ranking, excessive forms, or paid visibility.
- **CMO:** do not use a general feed as acquisition or retention; purposeful surfaces must first prove usefulness.
- **CDO/CTO:** provenance and completeness matter, but metadata cannot overwhelm the person; translate complexity through progressive disclosure.
- **COO:** closure standards are essential, but do not convert uncertainty into punitive user/employer scoring before baselines.

## 28. Decisions Required from Founder

1. Approve `Know / Discover / Act / Grow` as the Individual experience model.
2. Approve the five-item Individual information architecture.
3. Approve Minimum Useful Record instead of profile completion.
4. Approve Radar as calm action/closure state, not an application scoreboard.
5. Approve Purposeful Professional Layer and no infinite feed.
6. Approve Abhathli preview/confirm/no-mass-auto-apply policy.
7. Approve accessibility and Arabic-first journey tests as release gates.
8. Approve the free-core and no-paid-factual-visibility principle.

## 29. Research & Learning Appendix

### Core journey storyboard

1. Person states a goal or imports one source.
2. JID extracts proposals; person confirms facts.
3. JID immediately produces a useful output and explains privacy.
4. Opportunity detail shows truth, not just persuasion.
5. Abhathli explains relevance/gaps and offers bounded preparation.
6. Person chooses the action; native or external boundary is explicit.
7. Radar records state and next action.
8. Employer/email/user updates are source-labeled.
9. Outcome prompts reflection/evidence update, not public performance.

### Anti-slop experience gate

Reject any screen that exists to fill space, any number without a decision, any AI text without source/control, any notification without user value, any Arabic that reads as literal translation, and any social mechanic whose success metric is engagement volume.
