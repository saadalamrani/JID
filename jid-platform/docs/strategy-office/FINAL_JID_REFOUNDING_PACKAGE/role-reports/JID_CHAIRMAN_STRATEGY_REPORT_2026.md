# JID Chairman Strategy Report 2026

**Role:** Chairman / Strategy Office Chair
**Study date:** 2026-08-25
**Status:** Independent recommendation; not founder-adopted; no implementation authority
**Evidence labels:** `FACT`, `RESEARCH FINDING`, `MARKET SIGNAL`, `HYPOTHESIS`, `OPINION`, `RECOMMENDATION`

## 1. Executive Summary

**[RECOMMENDATION]** JID should be re-founded as a **Career Evidence & Opportunity Operating System**: Individual-owned career evidence connected to a source-traceable Opportunity Graph, Radar, a bounded verified-employer hiring loop, and later coverage-honest university outcomes. It should not present itself as a Saudi LinkedIn, a general ATS, or a national authority.

**[RECOMMENDATION]** Choose a **hybrid rebuild**. Preserve identity, Supabase/RLS/audit foundations, organization Directory/Profile separation, Arabic-first infrastructure, Catalog provenance, Lammah ingestion controls, and test assets; progressively replace fragmented career truth, static actor homes, opportunity navigation, packaging, and incomplete workflow grammar. The current locked non-production baseline contains substantial reusable controls and also an explicit record of partial release and unresolved defects. [S01–S04]

**[RECOMMENDATION]** For 90 days, permit only one proof loop: **Career Record → Opportunity Graph → Radar → bounded employer response/decision**. Test Abhathli as an explainable monitor, analyst, drafter, and preparation copilot. Do not permit autonomous mass application, fabricated fit scores, a general infinite feed, proprietary psychometric claims, or university outcome percentages without coverage.

The Chair’s decision is provisional because the academy’s apparent consensus was structurally correlated: every member was trained on the same proposed spine and ended with the same formal position. The strategy must therefore survive explicit alternatives, field evidence, economics, privacy/legal review, and founder adoption before implementation.

## 2. Mandate

The Chair owns the integrity of the decision system, not product feature selection. The mandate is to:

- frame company-level decisions and appoint one accountable lead;
- require independent positions before synthesis;
- preserve the strongest dissent and its reopen condition;
- separate facts, research findings, market signals, hypotheses, opinions, and recommendations;
- prevent non-production assets, code volume, fixtures, anecdotes, or founder observations from becoming traction;
- require economics, user value, data, operations, privacy, legal, and reversibility gates;
- return options to the founder without silently adopting them.

## 3. What I Learned

**[FACT]** JID has built more technical surface and governance infrastructure than it has proven market density, repeat individual utility, employer payment, or university data readiness. The current remote release record itself says the program was only partially shipped at its August 2026 checkpoint. [S01]

**[RESEARCH FINDING]** Saudi policy direction emphasizes human-capability development, career choices, labor-market foresight, private-sector participation, and better alignment of learning with labor-market requirements. This makes JID’s ambition relevant, but does not confer authority or adoption. [S06–S07]

**[RESEARCH FINDING]** Jadarat already has an official national mandate to unify and facilitate Saudi employment across public and private sectors. JID therefore needs a complementary wedge, not a replacement claim. [S08]

**[RESEARCH FINDING]** Global evidence favors richer evidence and structured skills assessment, while also requiring governance: WEF reports continued reliance on work experience and expected use of skills assessments; NIST frames AI trustworthiness as a lifecycle risk-management problem. [S09–S10]

**[MARKET SIGNAL]** The founder and attached research identify distrust, noise, application overload, ghosting, performative professional content, and weak closure. These are material hypotheses, not population estimates. [S18–S21]

## 4. Saudi Current State

**[FACT]** HRDF describes Jadarat as the national unified employment platform and primary enabler for employing Saudi workers, serving Saudi job seekers and public/private employers. [S08]

**[FACT]** GASTAT maintains current labor-market releases, including Q1 2026 statistics. This means JID should anchor national labor claims to official releases rather than invented mismatch percentages. [S05]

**[RESEARCH FINDING]** Vision 2030’s 2025 reporting and HCDP planning connect employment, skills, lifelong learning, data availability, and labor-market foresight. JID can contribute through better career and opportunity evidence, but only formal partnerships could make it public infrastructure. [S06–S07]

**[FACT]** Saudi PDPL gives data subjects rights to know, access, obtain, correct, and request destruction of data, limits processing and purpose changes, requires appropriate security, and provides for impact assessment and cross-border controls. JID’s career graph cannot be designed as an unrestricted institutional asset. [S11]

## 5. Global Benchmark

- **LinkedIn:** a professionally oriented feed using hundreds of profile, network, content, and activity signals; its feed also carries sponsored content. This proves the reach of a professional graph, not that engagement ranking is JID’s correct objective. [S12–S13]
- **Handshake:** demonstrates a connected network across individuals, employers, and educational institutions. Its value supports JID’s three-sided architecture, while its scale and US context are not directly transferable. [S14–S15]
- **HESA/NACE:** demonstrate that outcomes require an eligible population, known outcomes/response or knowledge rate, collection window, and defined denominator. [S16–S17]
- **MadsLorentzen/ai-job-search:** demonstrates an end-to-end local assistant pattern—profile, search, deduplication, tailoring, interview preparation, and outcomes—but its portal adapters, local-file model, fit scoring, and instruction-level security cannot be imported as a hosted Saudi product without redesign. [S22–S24]

## 6. User/Customer Jobs-to-be-Done

The strategic system must satisfy four distinct decision jobs:

1. **Individual:** understand what I can credibly show, find trustworthy opportunities, act without repeated re-entry, track what happened, and improve my next move.
2. **Employer:** publish a legitimate opportunity, request job-relevant evidence, make a structured decision, communicate closure, and learn where workflow quality fails.
3. **University:** define a cohort, collect lawful outcomes, see coverage and bias, and take program/career-service action.
4. **Public institution/partner:** obtain contracted, methodologically bounded insight—not a self-service claim of national truth.

## 7. Pain Points

- Fragmented career truth and duplicated CV/profile/application facts.
- Opportunity duplication, uncertain freshness, dead links, and weak source identity.
- High application activity without response or closure.
- Employer screening noise and inconsistent evidence.
- Static dashboards that display features rather than help complete a decision.
- Institutional desire for outcomes before identity, consent, and denominator readiness.
- Professional interaction that can reward visibility, performance, and engagement instead of utility.
- Strategic sprawl: many surfaces without one measured compounding loop.

## 8. Unmet Needs

The unmet need is not “more jobs” or “another profile.” It is a connected evidence chain:

`credible person facts → truthful opportunity → explicit action → employer response → outcome → updated career record`

Supporting needs include Arabic/English normalization, transparent reasons rather than fake percentages, finite professional knowledge, consent receipts, closure states, source health, and a safe path from personal utility to institutional insight.

## 9. Market Structure

JID enters a layered market, not a single marketplace:

- government employment infrastructure and programs;
- global professional networks and job platforms;
- direct employer career pages and ATSs;
- university career services/outcomes systems;
- assessment, interview, scheduling, and communication vendors;
- informal networks and human referrals.

**[OPINION]** JID cannot win through inventory scale or feature parity. It can attempt to win through Saudi-native evidence quality, source truth, workflow closure, and consent-governed linkages that make each completed journey improve the next.

## 10. Competitors/Substitutes

- **Jadarat:** official national platform; substitute for discovery/application in Saudi-only contexts, and a potential future partner—not a target for replacement. [S08]
- **LinkedIn:** professional identity, graph, content, recruiting, and advertising; JID should not compete on feed reach. [S12–S13]
- **Handshake:** three-sided early-career network and institutional workflow benchmark. [S14–S15]
- **Direct career sites/ATSs:** remain authoritative application endpoints for many employers.
- **CV tools and AI agents:** substitute for isolated drafting/search tasks but often lack JID’s proposed provenance, consent, and multi-actor workflow.
- **Human mentors, recruiters, and informal networks:** substitutes for interpretation, trust, and access.

## 11. Opportunities

1. A canonical Career Record that reduces repeated entry and supports truthful projections.
2. A Saudi Opportunity Graph with organization resolution, provenance, freshness, deduplication, and outcome state.
3. Radar as the user-controlled operational center for saved, applied, interviewing, offered, rejected, and closed states.
4. A narrow employer workspace for verified early-career hiring and closure.
5. Abhathli as an explainable copilot above Career Record and Opportunity Graph.
6. A Purposeful Professional Layer: profile-bound evidence, mentor knowledge, topic/event/opportunity discussions, finite digest—not a general infinite feed.
7. University outcomes only after cohort, consent, and methodology readiness.

## 12. Risks

- **Strategy risk:** a memorable category statement can outrun actor proof.
- **Cold-start risk:** three-sided ambition may diffuse the first wedge.
- **Data risk:** self-asserted facts, stale opportunities, and missing outcomes may look more certain than they are.
- **Trust risk:** employer monetization could become pay-to-win visibility or opaque ranking.
- **AI risk:** job content is untrusted input; generated claims and fit scores can mislead; automated decisioning can cause harm. [S10, S24]
- **Legal/privacy risk:** purpose changes, institutional sharing, retention, automated processing, and cross-border vendors require explicit governance and qualified review. [S11]
- **Execution risk:** clean-slate rebuilding could discard tested security/identity learning; incremental patching could preserve the wrong UX and data model.

## 13. What JID Should Do

**[RECOMMENDATION]** Adopt the Career Evidence & Opportunity OS as a testable company model. Establish five engines—Career Record, Opportunity Graph, Hiring Workflow, Outcomes Methodology, and Guidance/Knowledge—under one Trust/Governance layer.

**[RECOMMENDATION]** Run the first 90 days as one closed-loop validation program, with explicit go/iterate/stop criteria and no automatic feature carryover.

**[RECOMMENDATION]** Preserve exactly three public market actors for the first architecture: Individual, Business, University. Mentor remains an Individual capability; government is a partner/customer context.

## 14. What JID Should Not Do

- Claim national scale, endorsement, PMF, revenue, a moat, or outcome improvement without evidence.
- Build a general infinite feed or optimize time spent, likes, follows, or viral reach.
- Turn Abhathli into a mass auto-application agent.
- Sell personal data, covert ranking, or factual visibility.
- Build proprietary psychometrics or automated adverse employment decisions without scientific, legal, and operational ownership.
- Launch university percentages without eligible population, known outcomes, period, source, and bias caveat.
- Choose clean slate because it feels ambitious or incrementalism because code already exists.

## 15. Product/Service Implications

The product portfolio should be governed by a single question: does a capability create credible evidence, improve opportunity truth, enable a purposeful action, close a workflow state, or transform sufficiently covered outcomes into action? Capabilities failing that test are deferred or retired.

The initial service element is intentional: employer and university design partners need guided onboarding, methodology, and operating support before self-service scale.

## 16. Data Implications

- Define career facts by subject, assertion, source class, evidence, time, visibility, purpose, and revocation.
- Define opportunity records by source, organization resolution, fetch/verify time, expiry, duplicate group, and application authority.
- Treat application/outcome changes as auditable state transitions.
- Establish a metric registry with denominator and missing-data behavior.
- Never treat Directory coverage, seed events, or environment counts as adoption.

## 17. Technology Implications

**[RECOMMENDATION]** Hybrid rebuild:

- preserve Auth, Postgres, RLS, audit, Directory/Profile boundaries, Catalog/Lammah controls, i18n, and tests supported by repository evidence; [S01–S04]
- map the current schema to Career Record and Opportunity Graph contracts;
- build new actor homes and flows behind flags using expand/contract migration;
- do not permit a parallel Career Record truth store;
- isolate untrusted opportunity content from instructions and sensitive profile context;
- make all AI assistance observable, bounded, and reversible.

## 18. Privacy/Legal Implications

PDPL requires a documented lawful purpose, minimization, rights handling, security, retention, impact assessment, and controlled disclosure/transfer. [S11] Therefore:

- an Individual’s canonical record does not imply universal employer or university access;
- every employer projection needs a purpose and audience boundary;
- university analytics need cohort methodology and lawful collection/sharing;
- Abhathli must minimize external model/tool disclosure and clearly separate suggestions from user-authorized actions;
- licensed Saudi counsel must review high-impact automated employment, institutional sharing, and cross-border processing before launch.

## 19. Business Model

**[HYPOTHESIS]** Employer workflow, communication, structured evidence, integrations, and service-backed pilots are the first plausible payer layer. University outcomes services may follow after readiness. Individual premium may cover recurring utility such as advanced monitoring, preparation, or mentor transactions, but canonical identity, factual visibility, core discovery, and privacy controls should remain free.

No current WTP, price, CAC, retention, LTV, ARR, or revenue is claimed. Pricing begins as research, not entitlement inheritance.

## 20. Partnerships

- Verified employer design partners for a narrow early-career workflow.
- One university design partner for cohort/methodology readiness, not a dashboard sale.
- Authorized opportunity-source/API/feed partnerships before brittle scraping.
- Assessment and identity vendors where scientific or regulated capability should be integrated rather than invented.
- Government/public collaboration only through documented scope, authority, data terms, and public value.

## 21. Validation Needed

1. Compare Career Evidence & Opportunity OS against at least two alternative company theses.
2. Conduct structured research with Individuals across career stages, employers by buyer/user role, and university stakeholders.
3. Verify the current repo/schema/runtime and capability state at the implementation baseline.
4. Measure source freshness, deduplication quality, save-to-action, employer response/closure, repeated record use, and trust incidents.
5. Run privacy/data-flow, AI risk, and legal issue reviews.
6. Obtain at least one paid or economically explicit employer pilot before calling the wedge monetizable.

## 22. 90-Day Actions

### Days 0–30

- Founder provisionally selects the hypothesis and alternatives to test.
- Run Individual, employer, and university discovery with consistent protocols.
- Lock Career Record, Opportunity Graph, Radar, and employer-state contracts.
- Select 3–5 employer design partners and qualify two opportunity-source paths.
- Prototype Arabic-first home, Career Record, opportunity detail, and Radar.

### Days 31–60

- Deliver one non-production thin slice behind flags using preserved identity/security foundations.
- Connect truthful CV projection, source-traceable opportunity discovery, save/track, and basic employer response/closure.
- Establish event definitions, denominators, AI model/data cards, and human override.

### Days 61–90

- Run a bounded pilot with real consent and operating support.
- Pilot Abhathli as monitor/explainer/drafter/preparation assistant only.
- Decide go/iterate/stop for each capability; do not convert pilot existence into product proof.

## 23. 12-Month Roadmap

- **Q1:** validate and stabilize the closed Individual-employer loop.
- **Q2:** expand authorized opportunity sources, source health, employer workflow, communication, and structured work samples.
- **Q3:** launch a university outcomes design-partner workflow with coverage reporting; test mentor capability and finite knowledge surfaces.
- **Q4:** scale only proven segments; introduce integrations, pricing packages, and outcome-informed improvements with governance.

Every quarter ends with an evidence review and explicit retire/defer decisions.

## 24. 3-Year Direction

JID can become the Saudi operating layer for trustworthy career evidence and opportunity movement, with globally portable architecture and locally adapted taxonomies, programs, laws, and partners. By year three, a successful JID would show repeated Individual utility, native employer workflows, reliable Opportunity Graph coverage, methodologically honest university outcomes, and contracted ecosystem value. It would still not claim national representativeness without the sample and authority to do so.

## 25. Contribution to Saudi Leadership

JID could reduce search waste, improve opportunity provenance, strengthen early-career transition, support Arabic-first career navigation, improve employer closure, and create coverage-honest outcomes. These contributions align with human-capability and labor-market foresight priorities without duplicating Jadarat’s national mandate. [S06–S08]

## 26. Contribution to Global Potential

The portable innovation is not Saudi branding. It is the combination of user-owned career evidence, source-traceable opportunity intelligence, purposeful professional interaction, explainable assistance, closed hiring states, and honest outcome denominators. Global expansion should occur only after Saudi proof and with jurisdiction-specific legal, taxonomy, and source adapters.

## 27. Disagreements with Other Strategy Office Roles

- **With CEO:** I support the proposed thesis but reject adopting it before an alternative-thesis comparison and founder decision.
- **With CPO:** portfolio contraction is necessary, but a beautifully coherent portfolio can still solve an unvalidated problem; field gates precede roadmap confidence.
- **With UX/CX:** Individual dignity and utility are central, but user desirability alone cannot carry opportunity supply, employer closure, operational cost, or institutional legality.
- **With Business/CFO:** pilots and price anchors are evidence-generating instruments, not traction, ARR, or validated economics.
- **With CTO:** architecture risk matters, but technical completeness cannot delay the smallest reversible field test.
- **With CMO:** national-scale or “better than LinkedIn” language is prohibited until supported by measured outcomes and user choice.

## 29. Research & Learning Appendix

### Decision tests

- **Company test:** Is the problem large, repeated, and paid or strategically fundable?
- **Actor test:** Does each actor receive standalone value before full network density?
- **Evidence test:** What observation would falsify the recommendation?
- **Trust test:** Could the business model reward concealment, exposure, ranking, or manipulation?
- **Reversibility test:** Can the 90-day slice be stopped without identity/data harm?
- **Moat test:** Is value accumulating through lawful evidence, source quality, workflow outcomes, and institutional trust—or merely code volume?

### Change-of-recommendation conditions

The Chair would change course if field evidence shows that another wedge produces materially stronger repeat use, payer pull, trust, and defensibility; if canonical migration cost invalidates the hybrid plan; if authorized opportunity supply is insufficient; or if privacy/legal review makes the proposed linking model disproportionate.
