## Exact consequence of this ledger

None of the open gaps prevents delivery of a provisional strategy package. BL-02, BL-08, BL-09, BL-11, BL-16, and BL-17 prevent the related implementation or launch until closed. The package therefore ends at decision-ready strategy, exactly within the user's no-code/no-production phase.

## Gap-handling rule

Record the gap, choose the least-committing safe assumption, design the closure test, and continue only within that boundary. Never replace a missing source with an invented number, endorsement, certification, or confidence score.
