# JID 90-Day Evidence Sequence

**Status:** Recommended sequence, not an implementation authorization
**Day 0:** Founder adoption of the strategic direction and Constitution decision gate
**Principle:** Build only what converts a named uncertainty into evidence.

## Outcome at Day 90

JID should have one coherent, testable transition loop:

> canonical Career Record → explainable opportunity discovery → private Radar action → structured employer decision → consented outcome return

The target is not a public platform launch. The target is evidence that the loop solves real jobs for Individuals and a small set of Business and University design partners without breaching trust boundaries.

## Non-negotiable constraints

- No production SQL, production deployment, or public launch without a separate approved packet.
- Preserve exactly three public external actors: Individual, Business, University.
- Preserve Supabase RLS, Directory/Profile separation, auditability, Arabic/English parity, and real-source measurement.
- No mass auto-apply, fabricated fit score, emotion inference, personality rejection, paid organic ranking, or personal-data sale.
- Every pilot states purpose, cohort, duration, access, retention, exit, and success/failure criteria.

## Days 1–15 — Decide and instrument

### Founder decisions

1. Adopt or reject the proposed JID category and five-product system.
2. Select the first Individual transition segment and employer segment.
3. Approve the hybrid rebuild boundary.
4. Approve free-core, no-data-sale, and no-pay-to-rank covenants.
5. Decide the constitutional supersession process.

### Product work packets to authorize separately

- Canonical Career Record schema and evidence states.
- Opportunity Graph/Lammah provenance contract.
- Radar action/outcome event model.
- Employer requisition, structured screening, scorecard, and decision audit.
- Consent, access log, retention, export, correction, and deletion flows.

### Research and operating work

- Recruit 12–18 Individuals in the chosen transition segment.
- Recruit 3–5 employer design partners and one University discovery partner.
- Establish baseline task completion, time, error, trust, and support measures.
- Create product-level cost ledger, AI budget, and partner learning contracts.

### Gate 1

Proceed only if named design partners commit time, data rights are workable, the wedge has one accountable owner, and the constitutional/product boundary is resolved. Signed interest is a market signal, not product-market fit.

## Days 16–30 — Prototype the evidence contract

- Test bilingual Career Record creation from existing canonical profile data.
- Test evidence labels: self-asserted, source-linked, issuer-confirmed, platform-verified, expired/revoked.
- Test opportunity explanation: source, freshness, criteria, missing evidence, and user control.
- Prototype Radar as private, finite, and outcome-oriented.
- Prototype a structured employer screen using work samples/questions and anchored scorecards.
- Test University outcome definitions with explicit eligible cohort and known-status coverage.

### Gate 2

Proceed if participants understand evidence states, can correct data, complete the core flow, and report that recommendations are understandable. Stop or redesign if trust requires hiding provenance or if employer workflow depends on prohibited inference.

## Days 31–60 — Build a controlled pilot

Subject to a separately approved engineering packet:

- Implement the minimal Career Record contract on existing identity/RLS foundations.
- Expose governed Lammah records through an Opportunity Graph read model.
- Add Abhathli retrieval/explanation with explicit uncertainty and bounded actions.
- Add private Radar tracking and user-controlled reminders.
- Add one native employer requisition and structured review loop.
- Add consent receipts, access events, retention rules, exports, and correction paths.
- Instrument cost and evidence events; do not infer missing outcomes.

### Pilot scope

- One Individual transition segment.
- One employer workflow archetype.
- No broad social layer.
- University participation is methodology/discovery unless a safe cohort pilot is separately approved.

### Gate 3

Enter live controlled testing only after security/RLS, bilingual parity, accessibility, source provenance, model-output failure modes, and rollback are evidenced. A demo is not a gate pass.

## Days 61–75 — Run and observe

- Observe users completing real tasks without staff rescue.
- Review every recommendation error and employer override.
- Measure activation, repeated action, time saved, evidence completeness, workflow completion, support time, and AI cost.
- Interview non-completers and design partners.
- Record complaints, access requests, corrections, withdrawals, and near misses.

No employment outcome is attributed to JID without methodology and sufficient observation. Pilot participants are never marketing proof by default.

## Days 76–90 — Decide, do not decorate

Compile an evidence review with:

- observed behavior and denominators;
- qualitative counterevidence;
- security/privacy incidents and unresolved risks;
- partner continuation or refusal;
- cost per completed job and support burden;
- explicit hypotheses supported, weakened, or unresolved.

### Day-90 decisions

1. **Continue:** Core loop demonstrates repeat use, partner value, safe operation, and plausible economics.
2. **Narrow:** One side of the loop works; remove unsupported scope and run a second bounded test.
3. **Stop/reframe:** The problem, willingness to participate, trust model, or economics fail.

## Evidence scorecard

| Dimension | Minimum evidence form | Prohibited shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| Individual value | Completed tasks plus repeat behavior and interviews | Sign-ups or clicks alone |
| Employer value | Real workflow completion and continued-use intent/contract | Recruiter praise alone |
| University value | Methodology acceptance and usable cohort data | Logo or MoU alone |
| Trust | Comprehension, correction, access, withdrawal, incident review | Absence of complaints |
| Economics | Observed delivery/support/AI cost and paid-value signal | Competitor pricing |
| Outcomes | Cohort, period, numerator, denominator, source, coverage | Percent without denominator |

## Explicitly deferred

General social feed; likes/follower race; mentor payments; proprietary psychometrics; auto-apply; broad government dashboards; full ATS replacement; region-wide launch; credential-issuer network; predictive outcome scoring; marketplace commissions.
