# Individual Strategy

## Strategic promise

JID helps a person answer three recurring questions with evidence and action:

1. **Where can I go?** sectors, occupations, pathways, employers and real opportunities.
2. **What can I prove?** a reusable Career Record, credentials, projects and evidence.
3. **What should I do next?** a private, explainable action plan across search, applications, preparation and growth.

The Individual is not inventory for employers or universities. The person owns the record, controls disclosure and receives value before any organization joins.

## Priority segments

| Segment | Highest-value job | Initial product emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| Final-year student | Translate learning/projects into credible market evidence | Record setup, pathway discovery, internships/graduate programs, mentor guidance |
| Recent graduate | Find and navigate a first credible opportunity | Opportunity Graph, Abhathli, Radar, truthful CV and preparation |
| Early-career professional | Build direction and make a better move | evidence maintenance, career transitions, company/role research |
| Career changer | Explain transferable skills without false precision | skills mapping, gap explanation, learning plan, portfolio evidence |
| Mentor-capable professional | Share bounded expertise and offer services | mentor capability, articles, topic answers, booking and safety |

Executives and broad professional networking are later segments. The first 90 days should focus on final-year, graduate and early-career transitions.

## Core journey

```text
Goal and constraints
  → useful Career Record setup
  → sector / occupation / employer exploration
  → sourced opportunity discovery
  → save and compare
  → truthful CV/application preparation
  → explicit action or external application
  → Radar status and reminders
  → interview/assessment preparation
  → outcome and reflection
  → updated record and future direction
```

The product must deliver utility after the first few facts. “Complete your profile” is not a value proposition.

## Career Record

The record should distinguish:

- person-authored statements;
- organization/issuer-verified credentials;
- system-derived relationships;
- uploaded/private evidence;
- public, employer, university and private projections;
- source, date, status and visibility for each fact.

Use existing data boundaries where safe, but converge duplicate CV/profile/timeline values into canonical references. A generated CV is a user-approved snapshot, not a second career database.

## Discovery and career direction

Discovery should progress from broad to actionable:

- sector and occupation explanations in Arabic and English;
- Saudi employer and organization context from verified/provenance-rich records;
- required/evidenced skills without fake “fit” percentages;
- adjacent occupations and transition requirements;
- native and governed external opportunities;
- explicit constraints: location, work mode, eligibility, deadline and opportunity type.

ESCO and O*NET are useful global reference taxonomies, including Arabic ESCO labels, but JID requires a Saudi mapping layer and governance process. Neither taxonomy is Saudi labor-market truth by itself.

## CV and application assistance

Free:

- canonical record and basic bilingual CV formats;
- manual selection of truthful facts;
- PDF/export where reliable;
- application checklist and Radar.

Potential JID Plus:

- bounded tailoring suggestions to a specific opportunity;
- gap and evidence prompts;
- version comparison and quality checks;
- interview/assessment preparation;
- recurring Abhathli monitoring.

AI may rephrase or reorganize authorized facts. It must not invent experience, credentials, results, dates or skill level. Every generated change is reviewable before export or submission.

## Mentor model

Mentor remains an Individual capability. Approval grants a mentor designation, topics, service settings and operational tools; it does not create a fourth public actor.

Phase order:

1. verified expertise areas and a safe public mentor view;
2. request/accept/decline and structured session intent;
3. mentor articles and bounded topic answers;
4. scheduling integration;
5. paid sessions only after payout, refunds, tax/VAT, disputes, safeguarding and quality policies exist.

Reviews should not launch before completed-session proof, anti-retaliation rules, dispute handling and minimum sample rules. Do not show response/satisfaction percentages without enough real observations.

## Privacy and agency

- Private by default for evidence and search history.
- Purpose-specific sharing with employer/university/public audiences.
- Immediate revocation where technically and legally possible, with clear explanation of prior lawful processing.
- Separate owner and recipient payloads.
- No employer, university or algorithm can silently promote a self-declared fact to “verified.”
- No automatic application submission in the recommended Abhathli MVP.

## Reasons to return

Return loops should be event-driven, not addiction-driven:

- new opportunity meeting explicit constraints;
- deadline or Radar action;
- employer response or interview step;
- expiring/updated credential;
- mentor response or scheduled session;
- changed market requirement relevant to a selected goal;
- deliberate quarterly career review.

## Measures

Use event definitions and cohorts, not vanity activity:

- time to first useful record output;
- proportion completing one evidence-backed record section;
- saved opportunity followed by a declared action;
- fresh/source-valid opportunity rate;
- employer response coverage among declared applications where known;
- record reuse across CV/profile/application projections;
- privacy comprehension and revocation success;
- 30/60/90-day return for a career task;
- trust incidents, hallucination corrections and user-reported harm.

## What not to build now

- infinite feed, follower competition or public like counts;
- automatic mass applications;
- black-box match percentages;
- gamified profile completion;
- generic motivational content;
- proprietary credential verification without issuer relationship;
- salary claims without licensed/current market data and methodology.

## 90-day outcome

A consenting pilot Individual can create a useful Career Record, receive a truthful CV projection, find a source-valid opportunity, save/track it in Radar, prepare deliberately and understand exactly what JID did with their data.
