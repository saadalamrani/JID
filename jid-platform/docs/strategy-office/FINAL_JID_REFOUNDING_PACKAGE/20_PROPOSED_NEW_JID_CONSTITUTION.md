# Proposed New JID Constitution

**Version proposed:** 2026-08-25
**Status:** PROPOSAL ONLY — no legal, product, repository, production, or implementation authority until explicitly adopted by the founder through the repository amendment process.
**Supersession requirement:** Adoption must name the prior Constitution, record article-level changes, update binding repository instructions, and preserve an audit trail. Silence or publication of this file is not adoption.

## Preamble

JID exists to help people navigate career transitions with truthful evidence, useful opportunity intelligence, fair human decision processes, and accountable institutional outcomes. JID serves three public external actors—Individual, Business, and University—while respecting Saudi law, public institutions, human dignity, and user agency.

# Part A — Immutable Trust and Legal Principles

## Article A1 — Human dignity and agency

People are subjects with rights, not inventory. JID shall not coerce disclosure, exploit vulnerability, fabricate urgency, or make consequential employment decisions solely through automated systems.

## Article A2 — Truth and traceability

No metric, percentage, badge, verification, recommendation claim, institutional outcome, or marketing statement may be presented without a defined source, method, period, and limitation appropriate to the claim.

## Article A3 — Purpose limitation and minimization

JID collects and processes only data necessary for declared purposes. Access, reuse, retention, and sharing must be purpose-bound. Personal data shall not be sold.

## Article A4 — Security and privacy by architecture

Authorization is enforced at the data boundary, including row-level controls where applicable. Private data shall not be fetched to an unauthorized client and merely hidden. Secrets, production data, and privileged operations remain controlled and auditable.

## Article A5 — Actor rights

Individuals and authorized institutional users receive usable notice, access, correction, export, withdrawal/objection where applicable, deletion/retention handling, and a route to contest consequential errors. Material access to personal evidence is visible and attributable.

## Article A6 — Fair and accountable decisions

JID shall not use emotion, facial, voice, personality, or "culture fit" inference as an automated rejection basis. Assessments and decision aids must be job-relevant, documented, accessible, monitored, and subject to human review and accommodation.

## Article A7 — Accessibility and language parity

Arabic is a first-class product language. English parity is preserved. Accessibility, bidirectional layout, understandable terminology, and Latin digits in both languages are implementation requirements, not optional polish.

## Article A8 — Institutional and legal integrity

JID shall not imply government endorsement or official statistical authority without explicit lawful authorization. Saudi PDPL, cybersecurity obligations, contractual duties, intellectual property, source terms, and other applicable law govern every product and partnership.

## Article A9 — Reversibility and incident accountability

Material releases require evidence, rollback, auditability, and named ownership. Incidents, counterevidence, and user harm are recorded and resolved; absence of complaint is not proof of safety.

# Part B — Durable Product Principles

## Article B1 — Three public actors

The public actor model contains exactly Individual, Business, and University unless this Constitution is explicitly amended. Government may be a partner, customer context, regulator, or data authority; Mentor is an Individual capability. Neither is a default fourth public actor.

## Article B2 — Canonical personal evidence

The Career Record is the canonical source for personal career evidence. CV and application artifacts render or reference canonical data; they do not become parallel hidden sources of truth.

## Article B3 — Directory and Profile separation

Directory records describe discoverable entities or entries and are not owned profiles. Ownership, claims, and administrative authority require explicit lawful contracts; directory existence never grants profile control.

## Article B4 — Explainable opportunity intelligence

Opportunity records and recommendations expose material provenance, freshness, criteria, and uncertainty. JID may rank or guide only through documented, contestable, evaluated methods. Paid placement must never masquerade as organic relevance.

## Article B5 — Bounded professional contribution

Professional contribution may exist only when tied to a career task, evidence, learning, mentorship, cohort, or opportunity. JID shall not optimize a general feed for infinite engagement, public status competition, or fabricated engagement.

## Article B6 — Human-in-the-loop hiring

JID may structure questions, work samples, scorecards, and workflows. Final consequential hiring decisions remain attributable to authorized humans, with reason, override, retention, and contestability controls appropriate to context.

## Article B7 — Coverage-aware outcomes

University/institutional outcomes show eligible cohort, known-status coverage, numerator, denominator, period, source, exclusions, and limitations. Missing status is not silently converted into success or failure.

## Article B8 — Interoperability and exit

Where safe and lawful, JID supports export and documented interfaces so actors are not trapped. Partner integrations must define provenance, rights, revocation, source terms, and termination behavior.

# Part C — Current Strategic Decisions (Amendable by Recorded Founder Decision)

1. JID is provisionally defined as a Saudi career-evidence and opportunity operating system.
2. The product system is Career Record, Opportunity Graph, Hiring Workspace, Outcomes Exchange, and Guidance Network.
3. Lammah is the governed opportunity-ingestion and review capability.
4. Abhathli is the Individual-facing search/guidance capability; it does not mass auto-apply or claim guaranteed fit.
5. Radar is private action and outcome tracking.
6. The current build direction is hybrid rebuild: preserve proven trust foundations, rebuild incoherent experience and domain contracts.
7. The first delivery wedge is Career Record + Opportunity Graph/Lammah + Abhathli/Radar + one bounded hiring loop.
8. The Individual canonical core remains free; advanced recurring guidance may be paid. Business and University products use subscriptions/usage/implementation. Personal data is never a revenue product.

Each item is a current decision, not an immutable truth. Change requires a dated decision record stating evidence, owner, consequences, and superseded text.

# Part D — Experiments

Experiments require a hypothesis, actor/JTBD, cohort, consent, source/data plan, risk assessment, duration, success/failure threshold, owner, and rollback. Experiments do not create permanent rights to data or permanent roadmap commitments. Failed and negative results are retained.

# Part E — Implementation Standards

Binding engineering, design, data, security, research, deployment, and branch rules live in maintained operating documents and task packets. They must conform to Parts A and B, but are not constitutional merely because they are important. Current examples include strict TypeScript, `pnpm`, Supabase RLS, `next-intl`, Asia/Riyadh, bilingual parity, testing, non-production branches, and production approval gates.

# Part F — Documents That Are Not the Constitution

Roadmaps, product requirements, UX systems, pricing, metrics catalogs, source protocols, hiring policies, model choices, vendor lists, launch plans, task packets, and research reports are governed artifacts. They may change without constitutional amendment unless they conflict with Parts A or B.

# Part G — Governance and Amendment

## Article G1 — Authority

The founder holds final strategic adoption authority, subject to law and actor rights. Operational decision rights must be delegated in writing; consultation is not approval.

## Article G2 — Amendment packet

An amendment identifies the current article, proposed text, reason, evidence, affected actors, legal/privacy/security review, transition plan, dissent, approver, and effective date.

## Article G3 — Emergency action

Security, privacy, legal, or safety owners may pause a harmful capability immediately. Resumption requires evidence and recorded approval. Emergency action cannot be used to bypass permanent amendment.

## Article G4 — Annual review

Review this Constitution at least annually and after a material incident or business-model change. Review does not imply automatic amendment.
