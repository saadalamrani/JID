# Monetization Architecture

## Principle

Charge for recurring, measurable value and operational burden removed. Do not monetize personal-data sale, hidden ranking, artificial scarcity or career anxiety.

## Individual

### Always free

- canonical Career Record and privacy controls;
- basic public/private projections;
- basic bilingual CV creation/export;
- native and standard opportunity search;
- save/Radar and application declarations;
- core messages/status;
- data export/deletion rights.

### JID Plus hypotheses

- recurring Abhathli monitoring and multiple mandates;
- bounded truthful tailoring/version support;
- advanced interview/assessment preparation;
- career transition/pathway intelligence with explainable sources;
- premium mentor sessions/services, either transaction fee or subscription benefit later.

No paid boost of an Individual in employer search is recommended.

## Employer

| Revenue line | Value | Model |
|---|---|---|
| Starter workflow | opportunity, pipeline, communication, structured review | subscription or paid pilot |
| Growth workflow | team roles, automation, sourcing, analytics | subscription by workspace/usage band |
| Enterprise | SSO, integrations, program workspaces, governance/support | annual contract + implementation |
| Assessments | third-party tests/work samples | usage/pass-through + transparent margin |
| Programs | graduate/intern campaign operations | program fee |
| Employer brand | richer truthful authored Profile/analytics | subscription; never ranking |

Native opportunity publication may remain free or limited to support supply. Paid promotion, if ever tested, must be clearly labeled, separate from organic relevance and cannot affect candidate quality scoring.

## University

- paid readiness and methodology sprint;
- annual Outcomes Workspace contract by cohort/program scope;
- implementation/integration fee;
- career-service/employer-program modules;
- statistically responsible benchmarking only after network scale and comparability;
- custom reports/services with explicit methodology.

## Government/public sector

Only contracted pilots, program evaluation, interoperability or aggregate analytical services with explicit authority and procurement. Platform user data is not a saleable dataset.

## Pricing discovery

Do not publish recommended prices from this study. Use:

1. problem/budget/process interviews;
2. paid design-partner offers;
3. good/better/best package tests;
4. Van Westendorp or conjoint only with a defensible sample;
5. actual conversion, usage, support cost and renewal;
6. segment-specific pricing rather than one universal table.

## Unit economics ledger

Track separately by product:

- contract/subscription/usage revenue;
- assessment/vendor pass-through;
- payment fees and refunds;
- employer verification/review cost;
- support and success time;
- source ingestion/moderation cost;
- model/inference cost;
- university implementation/data operations;
- sales/procurement cycle;
- gross margin and contribution margin;
- renewal/expansion only after observed periods.

Formulas are definitions, not current claims:

- `Gross margin = (revenue - direct delivery cost) / revenue`.
- `CAC payback = acquisition cost / monthly gross profit from acquired cohort`.
- `Logo retention = retained eligible customers / beginning eligible customers`.
- `LTV` is prohibited until retention and contribution-margin history are credible.

## 90-day commercial experiments

- offer 3–5 employers a bounded paid or clearly priced pilot;
- test one university paid discovery/readiness sprint;
- interview Individuals on recurring Abhathli/preparation value, but do not interpret stated willingness as revenue;
- record delivery labor and vendor/model costs from day one;
- require a named buyer, problem, success method and continuation decision.

## Prohibited revenue

- personal-data brokerage;
- undisclosed advertising or paid reach;
- guaranteed interview/job outcomes;
- fake urgency and auto-renewal dark patterns;
- fees for basic privacy/data rights;
- employer access to non-consented university/Individual data;
- opaque automated ranking sold as intelligence.
