## 3. Coherence audit

The package consistently resolves:

- **Category:** career-evidence and opportunity operating system.
- **Actors:** exactly Individual, Business, University.
- **Products:** Career Record, Opportunity Graph, Hiring Workspace, Outcomes Exchange, Guidance Network.
- **Capabilities:** Lammah = governed ingestion; Abhathli = controlled Individual guidance; Radar = private action/outcome tracking.
- **Architecture:** hybrid rebuild, Postgres/Supabase-first, deterministic policy before AI.
- **Wedge:** Career Record → governed opportunity → Abhathli/Radar → bounded employer loop; optional separately bounded University diagnostic.
- **Social:** no general infinite feed; later finite purpose-bound experiment only.
- **Assessment:** structured work samples/scorecards owned by JID; validated specialized instruments integrated; human decision remains accountable.
- **Monetization:** free canonical Individual core; paid advanced guidance; Business/University workflow and implementation; no data sale/pay-to-rank.
- **Government:** complement/partner/customer context, not replacement, endorsement, or fourth actor.
- **Constitution:** proposal only until explicit founder adoption and repository supersession.

Role disagreements remain visible and have explicit conditional resolutions; they are not accidental contradictions.

**Coherence judgment:** PASS.

## 4. Anti-slop audit

### Writing and claim quality

- Placeholder/internal-reference scan found no standalone `TODO`, `TBD`, `PLACEHOLDER`, leaked internal web reference IDs, `example.com`, or lorem ipsum.
- Strategy terms map to defined product objects, actor jobs, decisions, or gates.
- Recommendations name what not to do, required evidence, and stop/narrow conditions.
- The package does not use AI, Vision 2030, “national,” “verified,” “skills-first,” or “moat” as unsupported decorative claims.
- Tables expose exact mappings where prose would hide denominator, owner, or decision boundaries.
- Roadmap language distinguishes direction from commitment and pilot from traction.

### Product/UX anti-slop

- No generic dashboard is accepted as the University product.
- No generic social feed is accepted as professional utility.
- No numeric match score is accepted as explainability.
- No chatbot shell is accepted as Abhathli's product definition.
- No scraped volume is accepted as opportunity quality.
- No feature count is accepted as current success or defensibility.
- UX direction is tied to Know/Discover/Act/Grow, progressive disclosure, Arabic-first use, accessibility, provenance, calm action, and correction/exit.

**Anti-slop judgment:** PASS.
