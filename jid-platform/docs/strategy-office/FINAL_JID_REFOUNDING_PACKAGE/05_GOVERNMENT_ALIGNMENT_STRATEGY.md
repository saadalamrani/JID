# Government and National Alignment Strategy

## Position

JID should be a **complementary private career and hiring infrastructure provider**, not a substitute for Jadarat, MHRSD, HRDF, the National Labor Observatory, GASTAT, education regulators or other public authorities.

Alignment means solving a documented need in a way compatible with national priorities and law. It does not mean displaying Vision 2030 language or implying endorsement.

## Areas of potential public value

- education-to-work transition and first-destination measurement;
- trusted opportunity and employer provenance;
- graduate/early-career hiring workflows;
- skills-demand and program-alignment signals with coverage caveats;
- regional/sector opportunity visibility;
- program evaluation for internships, training or employment initiatives;
- interoperable occupation/skill mappings;
- responsible AI and employment-process transparency.

## Engagement types

| Type | What JID may offer | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Public-source complement | link/normalize lawfully reusable opportunity/program data | source rights, provenance, freshness |
| Data partnership | exchange specific records/signals | formal agreement, lawful basis, minimization, security |
| Program pilot | recruitment/outcome workflow for a defined initiative | sponsor, cohort, success method, no inflated national claim |
| Analytical service | aggregate program/market insight | coverage, methodology, disclosure control |
| Infrastructure contract | workflow or interoperability layer | procurement, security, SLA, audit, continuity |
| Policy research | evidence from consenting/covered populations | sampling limits and non-representativeness disclosed |

## National-source discipline

Prioritize official sources such as GASTAT, MHRSD, HRDF/Jadarat, HCDP/Vision 2030, Ministry of Education, ETEC/NCAAA, TVTC, National Labor Observatory, SDAIA, DGA and NCA where their mandate applies. Record publication date, definition, population, update cycle and reuse conditions.

JID must not merge official statistics with platform activity as though they share a denominator.

## Government journey

1. Identify a specific program or policy question.
2. Map the responsible public entity and existing official capability.
3. Define JID's non-duplicative value.
4. Specify source/data/authority and what is unavailable.
5. Design a bounded pilot with an evaluation method.
6. Complete procurement, privacy, cybersecurity and contracting gates.
7. Report results with coverage and limitations.
8. Expand only after sponsor decision and evidence.

## Recommended first move

Do not pursue a broad national platform pitch in the first 90 days. Produce a **Saudi labor-market complementarity map** and use it to support one university/employer pilot. After product evidence exists, test a bounded conversation around graduate transition or opportunity-quality infrastructure with the appropriate institution.

## Claims JID may make only with evidence

- number/type of governed sources actually active;
- number of consenting pilot participants;
- pilot workflow outcomes within the defined cohort;
- source freshness/coverage;
- methodology and limits.

## Claims JID must not make

- government endorsement, mandate or integration without signed proof;
- national representativeness from JID users;
- official Saudization/localization compliance calculations without authority;
- replacement of Jadarat or an observatory;
- access to government data not actually granted;
- national impact projections presented as achieved results.
