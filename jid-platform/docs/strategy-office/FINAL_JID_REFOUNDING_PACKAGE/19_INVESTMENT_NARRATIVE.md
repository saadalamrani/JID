# JID Investment Narrative

## One-line thesis

JID is building Saudi Arabia's trusted career-evidence and opportunity operating system, connecting Individuals, Businesses, and Universities through consented records, explainable opportunity intelligence, structured hiring, and transparent outcomes.

## The problem

Career evidence is fragmented across CVs, profiles, certificates, applications, email, spreadsheets, and institutional systems. Individuals repeat work and face opaque discovery; employers struggle to assess consistently; Universities struggle to support transitions and report outcomes with clear coverage. Existing categories solve pieces but do not create a user-controlled evidence loop.

## The wedge

Start with a canonical Career Record that produces a truthful CV/application, connects to governed opportunity intelligence, guides a next action through Abhathli, records follow-through in private Radar, and supports one structured employer decision workflow.

This wedge is intentionally narrower than the long-term system.

## The system

1. **Career Record** — canonical, portable, evidence-bearing identity.
2. **Opportunity Graph** — governed inventory and provenance.
3. **Hiring Workspace** — structured, auditable Business workflow.
4. **Outcomes Exchange** — coverage-aware University outcome workflow.
5. **Guidance Network** — bounded human/professional contribution, added only when it improves transition tasks.

## Why Saudi Arabia

Saudi policy and institutions emphasize human capability, skills, participation, accountable digital services, and data protection. JID is not positioned as a public-platform replacement or unofficial statistics authority. The opportunity is a complementary workflow and evidence layer with genuine Arabic-first depth.

## Why now

**RESEARCH FINDING:** Employers globally report changing skill needs and growing use of skills-based signals, while professional platforms and AI tools increase information volume and opacity. **MARKET SIGNAL:** Modern recruiting vendors are adding AI workflow, assessments, and university-employer coordination. The opening is trusted synthesis and action, not more content.

## Product differentiation

- Evidence states and provenance instead of unsupported profile claims.
- Explainable guidance instead of a mysterious fit percentage.
- Private action/outcome tracking instead of engagement performance.
- Structured human decisions instead of black-box rejection.
- Cohort coverage and methodology instead of denominator-free outcomes.
- Arabic-first parity and Saudi institutional literacy.

## Business model

- Individual core: free; optional paid advanced recurring guidance.
- Business: subscription and usage bands plus implementation.
- University: annual outcomes/workflow subscription plus implementation.
- Government: explicit contracts only.

No personal-data sale, pay-to-win organic ranking, or employment guarantees.

## Defensibility

The candidate moat is a compounding, consented evidence graph plus trust operations, Saudi opportunity-source operations, cross-actor workflow standards, and outcomes learning with explicit coverage. None is claimed as proven today.

## Existing leverage

The current repository contains reusable identity/RLS/audit foundations, Directory/Profile separation, Arabic/English infrastructure, Catalog and Lammah ingestion work, CI/tests, and documented hardening. The strategy recommends a hybrid rebuild rather than discarding these assets.

## Evidence still required

No audited traction, retention, revenue, price, unit economics, or market-size model was available in the source package. The founder should not present invented TAM or forecast slides. The next investable milestones are real pilot usage, paid continuation, observed delivery cost, safe operation, and repeatable onboarding.

## 90-day milestones

- Founder and constitutional decisions closed.
- One transition segment and employer segment selected.
- Controlled end-to-end loop working under explicit trust gates.
- 3–5 employer design partners and one University discovery partner.
- Repeat-use, workflow, cost, and counterevidence report.
- Continue/narrow/stop decision.

## Use-of-funds logic

Capital should fund the evidence wedge, research/design, security/privacy, source operations, bilingual accessibility, design-partner delivery, and measurement. It should not fund broad social acquisition, proprietary assessment science, mass integrations, or geography before the gates pass.

## Key risks

Demand fragmentation; institutional sales cycles; low-frequency Individual use; source licensing/terms; assessment/AI liability; weak outcome coverage; high support/customization cost; incumbent response; and constitutional ambiguity. Each has an explicit gate in this package.

## Credible investor statement

JID's ambition is large, but the present claim is precise: a well-built evidence loop can reduce friction and improve accountability across Saudi career transitions. The 90-day program exists to test that claim before scale capital is justified.
