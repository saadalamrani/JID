# Purposeful Professional Layer Strategy

## Decision

Reopen professional interaction, but do **not** launch a general engagement-ranked infinite feed. The recommended product is a **Purposeful Professional Layer** in which every contribution is attached to a professional object, question, cohort, event or topic.

This is a strategic proposal only. The current repository's anti-feed rule remains an implementation constraint until explicitly replaced.

## Problem to solve

People need to:

- explain real work and learning;
- ask career questions;
- learn from mentors and practitioners;
- discuss an opportunity, event or discipline;
- celebrate credible milestones;
- build professional relationships.

The product should make these actions useful without making popularity the career signal.

## Product primitives

1. **Evidence-backed accomplishment:** structured milestone linked to credential, project, work or issuer where available.
2. **Project note/case study:** authored explanation attached to Career Record evidence.
3. **Mentor article:** long-form knowledge associated with approved expertise.
4. **Topic question/answer:** scoped professional knowledge with moderation.
5. **Cohort/event/opportunity room:** time-bounded conversation with membership/purpose.
6. **Comment/reply:** allowed only inside supported objects, with finite depth and reporting.
7. **Finite digest:** optional summary ordered by explicit relevance and recency, with an end.

## Incentive design

- no public follower count as status;
- no public like/reaction totals in the initial design;
- no paid organic reach;
- no engagement leaderboard;
- no streaks or infinite scroll;
- no “viral” objective;
- distribution follows topics, cohorts, explicit follows and moderation—not hidden popularity alone;
- contributor quality derives from evidence, useful accepted answers, completed mentor service and policy standing, with explanation and appeal.

## Trust and moderation prerequisites

- identity and organization representation rules;
- content scope and authenticity policy;
- reporting, blocking and appeals;
- spam, harassment, impersonation and fraud handling;
- moderation roles, queues, audit and SLAs based on measured volume;
- Arabic/English moderation capability;
- minor/young-person safeguards if applicable;
- copyright and defamation process;
- AI-generated content disclosure and anti-fabrication enforcement;
- finite retention and deletion rules.

## Rollout

### Experiment 1

Profile-bound projects and mentor articles with no comments. Measure authoring usefulness and reader actions.

### Experiment 2

Topic questions/answers in two bounded domains with trained moderation and finite pagination.

### Experiment 3

Comments inside mentor articles or cohort/event spaces. Measure resolution, reports and moderator cost.

### Experiment 4

Optional finite home digest only if prior objects create repeated professional value.

## Success measures

- useful object creation with evidence/source;
- question resolution or saved actionable guidance;
- profile/evidence updates caused by learning;
- mentor/service or opportunity action following content;
- moderation incidents per contribution and resolution time;
- low-quality/duplicate/AI-fabricated content rate;
- user control and feed-ending comprehension;
- repeat purposeful contribution, not session duration.

## Kill conditions

- engagement bait materially outgrows useful content;
- moderation cannot meet safety/Arabic coverage;
- public popularity begins influencing hiring visibility;
- creator incentives demand ads or paid reach;
- high plagiarism/fabrication or professional-harm incidence;
- no measurable career task or knowledge outcome after bounded pilots.
