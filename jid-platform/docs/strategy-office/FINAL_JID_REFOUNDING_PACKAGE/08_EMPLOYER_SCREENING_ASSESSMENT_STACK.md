# Employer Screening and Assessment Stack

## Decision principle

An assessment is justified only when the construct is job-relevant, the instrument is valid for the intended use and population, the process is accessible and fair, the candidate understands its role, and a human remains accountable for consequential decisions.

Reliability is not validity. Face plausibility is not criterion validity. A vendor label is not Saudi localization.

## Portfolio decision

| Category | Employer need | JID decision | Conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structured application questions | minimum requirements and evidence | Build | job-relevant, limited, auditable |
| CV/Career Record evidence review | compare relevant facts | Build | consented projection, no hidden inference |
| Structured interview kit | consistency and documentation | Build | anchored rubric, interviewer training |
| Work sample | demonstrate actual job task | Build workflow; employer owns content | scoring rubric, time burden, accessibility |
| Job-specific knowledge | confirm defined knowledge | Build limited workflow or partner | SME ownership, item security, review |
| English/Arabic language | role-specific proficiency | Integrate validated provider | CEFR/construct mapping, local use validity |
| Coding/technical execution | practical technical skill | Integrate | secure sandbox, plagiarism/AI policy |
| Cognitive/aptitude | broad learning/problem-solving | Partner/pilot only | psychometric validation, adverse-impact monitoring |
| Personality | development/team conversation | Avoid rejection use initially | no “culture fit” automation, qualified interpretation |
| Psychometric inventory | selected regulated use cases | Partner only | licensed instrument, local norms, counsel/psychologist review |
| Asynchronous video | scheduling and standardized response | Pilot workflow only | consent, retention, accessibility, no emotion inference |
| AI transcript/summarization | recruiter assistance | Pilot | source-linked output, human correction |
| Facial/voice/emotion analysis | alleged personality/fit | Avoid | insufficient trust/validity and high discrimination risk |
| Automated match/reject | efficiency | Avoid | unacceptable opacity and accountability in present model |

## Required assessment dossier

For every instrument/use case:

1. construct and intended decision;
2. target roles/population/languages;
3. content ownership and version;
4. reliability evidence;
5. content/construct/criterion validity evidence;
6. norm group and Saudi applicability;
7. accessibility and reasonable adjustment;
8. group-difference/adverse-impact monitoring;
9. candidate notice, consent/lawful basis and appeal;
10. scoring/threshold owner and change control;
11. proctoring/anti-cheating method and privacy cost;
12. retention, deletion and sharing;
13. human review and override;
14. vendor/security/cross-border terms;
15. ongoing outcome validation.

If the dossier is incomplete, the instrument cannot make a consequential recommendation.

## Scientific benchmark

TestGorilla's public materials distinguish content, construct, face and criterion validity and describe ongoing data collection; SIOP guidance stresses validation of AI-based selection procedures. JID should use these as diligence requirements, not as proof that any particular test is suitable for a Saudi role.

## Candidate experience

- disclose purpose, expected time and technology;
- avoid repeating equivalent tests across employers where lawful portability is possible;
- offer accessibility/accommodation route;
- provide practice/system check;
- explain result use and human decision authority;
- allow technical incident/report/appeal;
- give useful feedback when instrument rights and decision context allow;
- never surprise-record or infer emotion.

## Scoring governance

- employer cannot set arbitrary hidden cutoffs without owner and rationale;
- JID logs instrument/version/rubric and decision use;
- score is one signal, not truth;
- no cross-role reuse outside the stated purpose;
- no model training on candidate content without separate lawful basis and transparency;
- monitor differential completion, technical failure and pass rates with sufficient samples;
- pause on unexplained disparity, instrument drift or vendor evidence failure.

## 90-day scope

Build structured questions, interview scorecards and work-sample workflow. Select one language/skills assessment partner for diligence but do not integrate until security, legal, validity and candidate-experience gates pass. Do not build proprietary psychometrics.
