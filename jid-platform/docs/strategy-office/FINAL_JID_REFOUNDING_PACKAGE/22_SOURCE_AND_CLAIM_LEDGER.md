# JID Source and Claim Ledger

**Cutoff/access date:** 2026-08-25 unless stated
**Purpose:** Map consequential package claims to evidence and boundaries. This is not a bibliography-only list.

## Claim classification

- **FACT:** directly supported by an authoritative record within stated scope.
- **RESEARCH FINDING:** study/survey result with inherited method limits.
- **MARKET SIGNAL:** directional observation, not prevalence or causality.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** falsifiable proposition requiring validation.
- **OPINION:** role/stakeholder judgment.
- **RECOMMENDATION:** proposed decision based on evidence and uncertainty.

## Source-authority rules applied

1. Law/regulator/official administrative source for legal or Saudi institutional claims.
2. Current remote repository for present code/repository state; local stale checkout never overruled it.
3. Primary project repository/license/security/release for OSS claims.
4. Research paper/professional standard for empirical/scientific claims.
5. Founder record for intent and decisions—not for external facts.
6. Internal JID reports as synthesis/hypothesis sources—not independent proof.
7. Academy reports as role/governance material—not fourteen independent confirmations.

When evidence conflicts, the resolution and remaining uncertainty are recorded in `24_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md` and `26_BLOCKER_LEDGER.md`.
