# Employer Strategy

## Strategic promise

JID helps Saudi employers run **higher-signal, more respectful early-career hiring**: verified identity, clear opportunities, structured candidate evidence, consistent screening, timely communication and an auditable decision trail.

## Initial ideal customer profiles

1. Saudi employers hiring recurring graduate/intern cohorts.
2. Mid-market employers lacking a strong early-career workflow but able to provide a real hiring owner.
3. Large employers with a specific pilot program where JID can integrate rather than replace the enterprise ATS.

Avoid broad “all employers” positioning. A design partner must have a live hiring need, named owner, defined cohort, willingness to measure and authority to pilot.

## Employer jobs-to-be-done

- prove organizational identity and present a credible employer narrative;
- create a truthful, structured opportunity;
- reach relevant, consenting people without paid ranking corruption;
- understand candidate evidence consistently;
- reduce manual screening and coordination;
- run structured interviews/work samples;
- communicate status and close loops;
- learn which sources and process steps work;
- meet internal governance and localization needs without false compliance claims.

## Recommended owned workflow

```text
Verify organization representation
  → author Profile
  → create opportunity and evidence rubric
  → publish/distribute
  → receive consented candidate projection
  → review structured evidence
  → screen / interview / work sample
  → decide and communicate
  → retain lawful audit and outcome data
```

JID should own this workflow because it connects the other network sides. It should integrate with existing ATS/HRIS where enterprise replacement is unnecessary.

## Build, integrate, avoid

| Capability | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Organization verification/Profile | Build/reuse JID | core trust and identity boundary |
| Native opportunity authoring/distribution | Build | creates reliable supply and structured data |
| Candidate consent/projection | Build | differentiating trust boundary |
| Pipeline, communications, interview kits | Build a focused layer | necessary closed loop, not full HR suite |
| Enterprise ATS synchronization | Integrate later | procurement and workflow compatibility |
| General sourcing CRM | Limited build/integrate | avoid broad enterprise feature race |
| Work samples and employer-owned knowledge checks | Build workflow | job relevance and employer content ownership |
| Validated language/cognitive/psychometric tests | Partner/integrate | science, content, norming and liability burden |
| Coding execution | Partner/integrate | sandbox security and content maintenance |
| Facial/emotion/voice inference | Avoid | validity, discrimination, privacy and trust risk |
| Autonomous rejection | Avoid | human authority and appeal required |

## Candidate-quality philosophy

JID must not promise to “find the perfect candidate.” It should improve decision quality through:

- explicit job criteria;
- comparable evidence;
- structured rubrics;
- reasoned search/filtering;
- validated assessments when appropriate;
- documented human decisions;
- feedback and calibration.

“Quality of hire” is a delayed organizational outcome requiring role definition, performance measures and retention context. It cannot be inferred from a model score at application time.

## Saudi differentiators to validate

- Arabic/English opportunity and candidate evidence;
- graduate, internship, trainee and early-career program workflows;
- Saudi city/region and public-program context;
- organization verification and source provenance;
- skills/occupation mapping suitable for local job titles;
- respectful closure communication;
- employer/university cohort collaboration under explicit consent;
- reporting compatible with employer governance—not claims of government compliance.

## Employer product tiers — hypotheses

- **Pilot/Starter:** verified Profile, limited native opportunities, pipeline, basic communication and structured interview kit.
- **Growth:** team roles, candidate sourcing within consent, automation rules, analytics, integrations and usage allowances.
- **Enterprise:** SSO/security review, ATS integration, program workspaces, custom governance, support and aggregated intelligence.
- **Usage add-ons:** third-party assessments, messaging and verification services at transparent pass-through/markup.

Pricing is not validated. Test willingness to pay against a measured workflow outcome, not feature count.

## Pilot design

For each of 3–5 design partners:

- one live role/program;
- baseline process and pain;
- explicit selection rubric;
- agreed candidate communication standard;
- source/consent boundaries;
- measured stage completion and time;
- weekly operational review;
- end-of-pilot decision and evidence pack.

Do not use pilot logos publicly without permission or describe design partners as customers unless a valid commercial relationship exists.

## Measures

- opportunity completeness and publish time;
- eligible applicant/candidate projection coverage;
- stage decision time;
- candidate closure/communication rate;
- structured rubric completion;
- interview no-show/cancellation and reschedule handling;
- recruiter time per reviewed candidate;
- assessment completion/appeal/accessibility incidents;
- paid pilot conversion and renewal only when actual;
- cross-tenant/privacy/security incidents.

## What not to do

- paid ranking disguised as relevance;
- black-box match or employability scores;
- unrestricted CV scraping or resale;
- blanket talent-pool consent;
- personality/culture-fit rejection without defensible evidence;
- enterprise ATS scope before the wedge is proven;
- implied Saudization/legal compliance without authoritative calculation and review.
