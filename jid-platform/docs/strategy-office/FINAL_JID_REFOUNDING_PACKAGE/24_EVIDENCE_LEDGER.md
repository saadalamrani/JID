## Evidence exclusions

- The Academy 14-report consensus is not counted as fourteen independent sources; the reports are substantially anchored to a common source package.
- The combined labor report is not counted in addition to its five component reports.
- Founder intent is not used as proof of market demand.
- Repository shipping reports establish repository evidence, not independently observed production parity.
- OSS license does not grant permission to scrape third-party sources.
- A policy target is not a realized outcome.
- A rate without cohort/period/denominator/method is not accepted.
