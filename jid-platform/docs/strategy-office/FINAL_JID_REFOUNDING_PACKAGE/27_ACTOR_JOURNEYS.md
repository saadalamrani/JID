# JID Actor Journeys

## Journey design rules

There are exactly three public external actors: Individual, Business, University. Government appears below only as an ecosystem authority/partner context. A Mentor acts through an Individual capability with scoped permissions. Each journey begins with a real job, exposes evidence and access, ends in a next action/outcome, and provides correction/exit.

## Individual — From fragmented history to accountable next step

| Stage | Individual job | JID experience | Evidence/rights | Success signal | Failure to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Orient | Understand what JID does and why data is needed | Choose a transition goal; see plain-language purpose and free-core boundary | Purpose/legal basis notice; no forced broad sharing | Correct mental model | “AI finds you a job” promise |
| 2. Build | Stop re-entering the same history | Create/import canonical Career Record; confirm facts and presentation | Evidence state, source, date, visibility, correction | Complete reusable record | Parser becomes hidden truth |
| 3. Render | Produce a truthful CV/application | CV Builder projects selected canonical facts and preferences | No parallel data store; export available | Usable bilingual/ATS-readable artifact | LLM invents or silently edits facts |
| 4. Discover | Find relevant, current opportunities across channels | Abhathli searches governed Opportunity Graph/Lammah | Source, freshness, constraints, reason codes, uncertainty | Qualified save/action | Fake fit percentage; expired/unsafe link |
| 5. Prepare | Know gaps and next action | Evidence checklist, truthful tailoring, questions, interview preparation | User review before external use | Better-prepared action | Fear/urgency manipulation |
| 6. Act | Apply or respond through an authorized channel | User deliberately chooses external/native action | No mass auto-submit; log actual state | Completed authorized action | Agent sends without authority |
| 7. Track | Remember follow-ups without public performance | Private Radar states, reminders, notes, withdrawal | Private by default; notification control | Repeated useful update | Social comparison or notification pressure |
| 8. Learn | Record outcome and update evidence | Outcome, employer feedback where lawful, new evidence | Correction/appeal; source and audience | Improved next transition | Unknown treated as rejection/success |
| 9. Control/exit | See who accessed data and leave | Access history, grants, export, withdrawal/deletion/retention path | Usable rights operation | Rights task completes | Dark patterns or stranded data |

### Critical moments

First evidence label, first recommendation explanation, first employer access, first rejection/unknown outcome, first correction, and export/exit. Trust can be lost at any one of them.

## Business — From requisition intent to consistent accountable decision

| Stage | Business job | JID experience | Evidence/control | Success signal | Failure to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Establish authority | Prove organization and authorized staff | Business workspace and roles separate from Directory | Verification scope/date; least privilege | Authorized accountable workspace | Directory record claimed as Profile |
| 2. Define need | Translate role into job-relevant criteria | Structured requisition, must/prefer, evidence request, location/mode, lifecycle | Criteria version; source and owner | Clear, publishable requisition | Vague “culture fit” |
| 3. Publish/source | Reach candidates lawfully | Native posting and/or approved inventory connector | Provenance, expiry, apply route, source terms | Fresh controlled vacancy | Duplicate/ghost/unsafe source |
| 4. Screen | Reduce noise consistently | Knockout eligibility only where lawful; work sample/questions; anchored scorecard | Construct/use, accommodation, reviewer audit | Completed structured reviews | Black-box auto-reject |
| 5. Interview | Gather comparable evidence | Guide, evidence capture, calendar/video integration | Consent/recording/retention; no emotion inference | Consistent evidence | Unstructured subjective notes |
| 6. Decide | Make an attributable human decision | Stage transition with reason and override | Actor/time/reason/history | Timely documented decision | Silent disappearance |
| 7. Close | Inform candidates and close vacancy | Status, communication, expiry/closure discipline | Purpose-bound message; audit | Closed loop | Stale “open” vacancy |
| 8. Learn | Improve process and quality | Aggregate funnel, time, completion, overrides and outcomes | Denominators/source/coverage; no unsupported score | Actionable process change | Vanity dashboard |
| 9. Exit | Export and end contract safely | Data export, role revocation, retention/termination | Contract and user-rights preservation | Clean offboarding | Lock-in or orphan access |

## University — From cohort uncertainty to evidence-led intervention

| Stage | University job | JID experience | Evidence/governance | Success signal | Failure to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Define authority | Establish institutional purpose, cohort, buyer and users | University workspace, roles, agreement, data map | Controller/processor, legal basis, approved purpose | Signed workable governance | MoU used as product validation |
| 2. Define cohort | Know who is eligible and in scope | Cohort `E`, period, inclusion/exclusion and source mapping | Versioned cohort definition | Reproducible denominator | Upload list treated as truth without provenance |
| 3. Invite/support | Help students complete evidence/actions | Purpose-specific invitation; student-controlled Career Record and service plan | No blanket employer access; withdrawal and correction | Useful participation and known coverage | Coercive consent through University |
| 4. Intervene | Route services before/after graduation | Segment by observed need/action, not opaque employability score | Authorized staff view; minimum necessary data | Completed intervention | Labeling students as low-potential |
| 5. Engage employers | Capture structured opportunity/feedback | Approved employer events, program/skill evidence, feedback instrument | Source, response coverage, method | Actionable program/career-service insight | Sparse opinions become ranking |
| 6. Measure | Report outcomes honestly | Show E, K, N, K/E, chosen rate, period, source, missingness, limitation | Reproducible metric catalog | Decisions improve | Denominator-free success claim |
| 7. Align | Compare with official context | NLO/ETEC/GASTAT context linked and labeled | No merging of incomparable sources | Proper contextual interpretation | Claim national authority |
| 8. Improve/renew | Decide program/service action | Intervention review, employer feedback, renewal evidence | Decision and method audit | Demonstrated workflow value | Dashboard without action |
| 9. Exit | End access and preserve actor rights | Export aggregates/authorized records; revoke staff; enforce retention | Individual rights survive contract end | Clean offboarding | University contract overrides Individual rights |

## Government/institution ecosystem journey — not a fourth public actor

1. Identify mandate and authoritative owner.
2. Define whether the relationship is policy consultation, data/source permission, contracted service, verification, or research.
3. Establish written legal authority, data terms, classification, security, publication, retention, and audit.
4. Use official identifiers/methods and label JID operational data separately.
5. Deliver agreed public/institutional value without implying endorsement.
6. Terminate or renew under contract, preserving Individual and partner rights.

## Mentor capability journey

An Individual may qualify or be authorized to mentor, publish an evidence-linked article, answer a bounded question, or join a cohort/event room. The mentee chooses contact and disclosure. Expertise/verification claims show source and date. No follower race, public popularity score, guaranteed outcome, or unscoped private access.

## Cross-journey handoffs

| Handoff | Required object | Forbidden shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| Individual → Business | Deliberately shared Career Record projection/application | Employer browses all private Profile data |
| Business → Individual | Attributable stage/outcome/feedback event | Inferred outcome or silent state change |
| Individual → University | Purpose-specific service/outcome grant | Enrollment implies blanket consent |
| University → Individual | Intervention invitation/context | Institution overwrites canonical evidence |
| Source → Opportunity Graph | Immutable provenance and freshness | Unsourced scraped record |
| Outcome → Analytics | Coverage-aware, purpose-permitted event | Missing/biased data turned into ranking |
