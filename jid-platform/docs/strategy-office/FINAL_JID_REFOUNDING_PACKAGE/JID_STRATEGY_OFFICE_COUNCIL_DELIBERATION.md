# JID Strategy Office Council Deliberation

**Council date:** 2026-08-25
**Council:** Chairman, CEO, Business & Partnerships, Universities, Government Relations, UX/CX, CTO, CPO, CDO, COO, CMO, CFO, GRC, CLO
**Status:** Decision recommendation, not founder adoption or implementation authority

## 1. Council method and independence caveat

The fourteen roles reviewed shared founder, research, repository, Academy, UX/brand, Saudi primary-source, global benchmark, and GitHub/OSS inputs. Each produced the same 30-section contract, then stated role-specific dissent and founder decisions.

The Council explicitly rejects a false claim of fourteen independent confirmations. The Academy reports were structurally correlated around a shared prior thesis. Consensus here means the recommendations survived role-based challenge and common evidence; it does not mean the market has validated them.

## 2. Source-authority resolution

For strategy, the founder mandate may reopen previously closed questions. For implementation, the current repository Constitution remains binding until explicitly superseded. Current remote repository truth outranked a stale local checkout; Saudi official sources outranked internal claims about institutions; primary repositories/licenses/security pages outranked OSS summaries; internal reports generated hypotheses, not external facts.

## 3. Alternative theses tested

| Thesis | Strongest case | Council judgment |
|---|---|---|
| Another job board/aggregator | Familiar demand, direct listings, simple acquisition story | **Rejected as core.** Jadarat and global platforms already operate at scale; listing volume does not solve evidence/closure |
| Saudi LinkedIn/professional network | Identity, content, mentorship, referrals and engagement can create network effects | **Rejected as core.** Broad feed/status mechanics dilute the transition job and add moderation/trust burden; bounded professional contribution remains an option |
| University outcomes/analytics company | Clear institutional pain and annual contracts | **Rejected as sole identity.** NLO/ETEC already occupy official outcomes terrain; JID can add intervention and coverage-honest operations |
| AI job-search/auto-apply agent | Immediate Individual appeal and visible automation | **Rejected in autonomous form.** Source rights, spam, fabricated signal and consequential-action risk are incompatible with JID trust |
| General ATS/HR suite | Employer payer and established category | **Rejected.** It imports broad HR scope and loses the cross-actor Career Record thesis |
| Career Evidence & Opportunity Operating System | One reusable evidence loop can serve transition, hiring and outcomes | **Selected provisionally.** Best synthesis, but demand, price, repeat use and outcome effects remain hypotheses |

## 5. Motions and deliberation

### Motion 1 — Adopt the OS thesis

**For:** All roles accepted Career Evidence & Opportunity OS as the strongest provisional direction.
**Qualification:** Chairman and CDO insisted it not be called a proven category or data moat. Government Relations required “private complementary platform” wording in public-institution contexts.
**Resolution:** **PASS, provisional.** Marketing category and government-facing description may differ in precision, not substance.

### Motion 2 — Retain exactly three public actors

**For:** Unanimous. Government is an authority/partner/customer context; Mentor is an Individual capability.
**Resolution:** **PASS.** Any fourth-actor change requires explicit constitutional and architecture amendment.

### Motion 3 — Choose hybrid rebuild

**For:** Chairman, CEO, CTO, CPO, CDO, COO, GRC, CFO and others favored preserving proven trust foundations while rebuilding incoherent domain/experience contracts.
**Tension:** CEO/Business warned against perfect ontology before a thin slice; CTO/CDO warned against semantics-free shortcuts.
**Resolution:** **PASS with thin-contract rule.** Every thin slice has minimum owned semantics, owner, migration/retirement path and audit; no full ontology or greenfield rewrite is required before learning.

### Motion 4 — First 90-day loop and University timing

**Core consensus:** Career Record → Opportunity Graph/Lammah → Abhathli/Radar → bounded employer response/decision. Three to five employers; no broad launch.
**Dissent:** Universities role argued for a paid 12–16 week diagnostic in parallel, not after the employer product is fully proven. CFO/COO worried about capacity and bespoke service; CEO accepted University readiness but sequenced institutional intelligence later.
**Resolution:** **PASS with conditional parallel track.** One University readiness/paid-diagnostic track may run in parallel if it uses separate capacity, no production integration, bounded data, reusable method, fixed subsidy, and no dashboard/outcome claims. It is discovery/service evidence, not a second product build.

### Motion 5 — Abhathli and AI authority

**For:** Abhathli may retrieve, explain, compare, monitor, prepare, draft and rehearse over authorized data.
**Against:** Mass auto-apply, source evasion, silent external messages, mutation of canonical facts, fabricated fit/employability scores, emotion/personality inference, and autonomous rejection.
**Resolution:** **PASS.** Deterministic policy and explicit human authority gate every external or consequential action; bilingual evaluation and incident/appeal are required.

### Motion 6 — Professional/social capability

**Options debated:** permanent prohibition; general feed; bounded Purposeful Professional Layer.
**Dissent:** CMO/UX/CPO see possible utility in projects, mentor articles, Q&A and cohort/event rooms; GRC/CLO/CFO emphasize moderation, rights, fraud, crisis and cost; CTO rejects importing a social foundation.
**Resolution:** **PASS to defer general social; preserve bounded experiment option.** No infinite feed, follower/like race, public popularity market, paid organic reach, or feed-led acquisition. A later finite experiment must prove task utility and pass interaction/moderation gates.

### Motion 7 — Assessment and hiring decisioning

**For:** JID owns structured questions, work-sample workflow, anchored scorecards, audit and human decision state. Specialized language/coding/cognitive/psychometric tools are integrated only with validation and contract evidence.
**Resolution:** **PASS.** No proprietary psychometric suite in the wedge; no emotion/facial/voice inference; no black-box progression/rejection; provide accommodation, human review and contestability.

### Motion 8 — Monetization

**For:** Canonical Individual record/discovery/basic tracking remain free; advanced recurring guidance can be paid after value/frequency proof; Business and University pay subscription/usage/implementation; Government revenue is contract-only.
**Dissent:** Business accepts subsidized service-backed pilots if learning is capped; CFO rejects calling them traction or scalable margin. Universities wants a paid diagnostic sooner.
**Resolution:** **PASS.** Every subsidy has objective/cap/conversion date; no data sale, urgency exploitation, employment guarantee, or pay-to-win ranking.

### Motion 9 — Government and official-source posture

**For:** Complement Jadarat/NLO/ETEC/GASTAT/MHRSD/HRDF; use official definitions and authorized sources; no endorsement or official-status claim.
**Resolution:** **PASS.** Government is excluded from the Year-1 base-case revenue plan until a named authority, budget, procurement path, contract and data terms exist. Listening outreach requires an authorized representative and claims protocol.

### Motion 10 — Outcomes and data moat claims

**For:** Outcomes must display eligible cohort, known-status coverage, numerator, denominator, period, source, exclusions, missingness and limitations.
**Resolution:** **PASS.** JID may call the evidence graph a moat-building path, never a proven proprietary data moat until lawful repeat use, coverage, correction quality and measurable cross-workflow utility exist.

## 6. Council consensus

The Council recommends:

1. JID as a provisional Saudi career-evidence and opportunity operating system.
2. Exactly three public actors.
3. Five connected products with one Career Record-led evidence system.
4. Hybrid rebuild preserving trust foundations and replacing product/domain coherence progressively.
5. A 90-day early-career transition/employer proof loop, with an optional separately bounded University diagnostic.
6. Lammah as governed ingestion; Abhathli as controlled guidance; Radar as private action/outcome tracking.
7. Purposeful professional contribution only after the wedge and interaction gates.
8. Structured, human-accountable assessment/hiring; specialized high-risk instruments integrated, not improvised.
9. Free Individual core; paid advanced guidance; Business/University workflow revenue; no data sale/pay-to-rank.
10. Explicit constitutional adoption before implementation authority changes.

## 7. Preserved dissent

- **Sequencing:** Universities wants a parallel paid diagnostic; CEO/CFO/COO constrain it to separate capacity and no product/dashboard expansion.
- **Speed vs semantics:** CEO/Business favor rapid reversible pilot; CTO/CDO require minimum canonical contracts from the first slice.
- **Professional layer:** UX/CMO/CPO preserve bounded utility; GRC/CLO/CFO favor strong deferral until moderation and economics exist.
- **Government wording:** CEO/CMO category can say “for Saudi Arabia”; Government Relations requires “private complementary” language in official contexts.
- **Pilot economics:** Business accepts temporarily poor margin for learning; CFO requires explicit caps and refuses traction claims.
- **Legal vs risk:** CLO defines legal floor; GRC may still reject legally permissible uses with unacceptable residual trust/fairness risk.

None of these disagreements blocks the provisional strategy because each has an explicit gate and decision owner.

## 8. Matters reserved to the founder

The Council cannot adopt the Constitution, select the first segment, set the capital envelope, authorize external institutional representation, accept critical residual risk, approve a source/data contract, or authorize implementation/production. Those choices are itemized in `JID_FOUNDER_STRATEGIC_DECISION_MEMO.md`.

## 9. Final Council judgment

JID should pursue a large systems ambition through a small, evidence-bearing loop. The company wins only if career evidence becomes genuinely useful across Individual action, employer decision, and University intervention while the person retains agency and the institution can trust the method. The Council therefore recommends ambition in architecture, restraint in claims, and strict sequencing in capital and risk.
