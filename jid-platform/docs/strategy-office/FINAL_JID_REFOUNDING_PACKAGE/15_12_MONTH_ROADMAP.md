# JID Twelve-Month Roadmap

**Status:** Conditional roadmap. Each horizon begins only after the previous evidence gate; dates are not promises.

## Year-one thesis

JID earns the right to expand by making one Saudi career transition materially clearer and more trustworthy for Individuals, while reducing evidence and coordination burden for a small employer network. University outcome capability follows the same evidence contract; it is not a disconnected dashboard business.

## Quarter 1 — Prove the transition loop

Deliver the sequence in `14_90_DAY_SEQUENCE.md`. Establish the Career Record, Opportunity Graph, Abhathli, Radar, and a bounded hiring workflow. Decide whether the wedge continues, narrows, or stops.

**Exit evidence:** repeat task use; partner continuation signal; acceptable trust/safety performance; cost baseline; signed decision log.

## Quarter 2 — Make the wedge repeatable

- Standardize employer onboarding and requisition templates.
- Improve evidence portability and truthful CV/application rendering.
- Expand opportunity sources only through provenance and freshness gates.
- Add user-visible recommendation feedback and correction loops.
- Run University outcomes methodology with one bounded cohort if data rights and coverage are adequate.
- Introduce paid employer/university pilots and test an Individual premium boundary without degrading the free core.

**Exit evidence:** a second cohort/partner can onboard without founder heroics; implementation hours decline; repeat use persists; paid continuation is observed.

## Quarter 3 — Deepen evidence and institutional workflow

- Add approved issuer/source confirmations where useful.
- Add reusable work-sample and interview scorecard templates.
- Publish outcome methodology and coverage conventions.
- Launch bounded professional contribution: profile-bound projects, mentor articles, topic Q&A, or cohort rooms—only if moderation and purpose tests pass.
- Improve partner APIs/exports before adding custom integrations.

**Exit evidence:** contribution improves transition tasks without feed-driven harm; institutional reports remain reproducible; partner expansion is not dominated by custom work.

## Quarter 4 — Decide the platform boundary

- Evaluate second Individual transition segment or employer vertical.
- Evaluate a standardized University annual product.
- Establish repeatable pricing, support, and renewal motions.
- Consider selected credential interoperability.
- Prepare regional research only if Saudi retention, trust, and unit economics pass.

**Exit evidence:** one repeatable acquisition channel per paying actor, renewing value, product-level gross-margin path, and no unresolved high-severity trust defect.

## Workstream view

| Workstream | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Career Record | Minimum evidence contract | Portability and correction | Issuer/source confirmations | Interoperability decision |
| Opportunity Graph/Lammah | Governed read model | More approved sources | Quality/freshness operations | Selective partner access |
| Abhathli | Explain/prepare/track | Feedback and recurring guidance | Contextual preparation | Segment expansion decision |
| Radar | Private action/outcome loop | Reminders and workflow refinement | Cross-transition history | Outcome learning review |
| Hiring Workspace | One structured workflow | Repeatable onboarding | Template library | Vertical/segment decision |
| Outcomes Exchange | Methodology only | One bounded cohort | Reproducible workspace | Annual product decision |
| Guidance Network | Deferred | Research/moderation design | Bounded experiment | Keep, narrow, or stop |

## Operating capabilities required

- Product council with explicit decision rights.
- Research repository separating claims, assumptions, and decisions.
- Data governance owner and access/retention operations.
- Model evaluation and incident process.
- Design-partner success motion distinct from custom consulting.
- Product-level finance and capacity planning.
- Arabic/English content operations and accessibility review.

## Year-one non-goals

National labor statistics authority; public-sector system replacement; mass social network; general-purpose ATS/HRIS; automated hiring decisions; mass auto-apply; broad mentor marketplace; proprietary psychometric claims; global consumer launch.

## Roadmap governance

Every proposed addition states: actor/JTBD, observed evidence, owner, data source, privacy/security impact, cost, success/failure threshold, rollback, and which existing priority it displaces. Nothing enters the roadmap because it is fashionable or because a source document mentioned it.
