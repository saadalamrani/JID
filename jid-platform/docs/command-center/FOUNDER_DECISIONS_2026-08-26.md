# JID — Founder Decisions — 2026-08-26

**Status:** Adopted decisions
**Purpose:** Compact founder authority record for Wave 1 and later task packets

This file records decisions explicitly adopted by the founder during the 2026-08-26
refounding discussion. It is not a research report and must not be treated as evidence that
unvalidated market, revenue, network, or outcome claims are already true.

## 1. Public definition

> جِد منصة مهنية سعودية تربط الفرد بالفرصة، وجهة التوظيف بالكفاءة، والجامعة بمخرجاتها.

Operating shorthand:

- Individual -> Opportunity
- Employer -> Talent / capability
- University -> Graduate outcomes / graduates in the labor market

## 2. Actor model

Exactly three public actors:

1. Individual
2. Business / Employer
3. University

Mentor is an Individual capability. Government is a partner/customer/authority/program
context, not a fourth public marketplace actor.

## 3. Build strategy

Adopt **Hybrid Rebuild**. Preserve trustworthy identity, security, privacy, RLS/audit,
Directory/Profile, i18n, CI/test, and other foundations only where current evidence supports
them. Rebuild fragmented or strategically obsolete journeys/contracts progressively.

Delivery uses governed waves rather than a binding calendar.

## 4. Initial segment

Start with Saudi final-year students, recent graduates, and early-career professionals,
paired with employers hiring graduate and early-career talent. This is a starting segment,
not a permanent market boundary.

## 5. AI authority

Adopt **Assistive + Explainable + Human-Authorized** AI.

AI may search, retrieve, summarize, compare, draft, prepare, rehearse, monitor, explain,
recommend, and track within authorized permissions.

Do not allow mass autonomous applications, fabricated fit/employability percentages,
autonomous final hiring/rejection, facial/voice/emotion employment inference, silent Career
Record mutation, or consequential external action without authorized human control.

## 6. Professional / Social Layer

The Professional / Social Layer is approved. Posts, comments, follows, reactions, articles,
achievements, mentor content, and organization/university publishing may exist in a
professionally relevant feed.

Approved lightweight post contexts:

`مشاركة / إنجاز / مشروع / مقال / سؤال / شهادة / فرصة`

Ordinary posts never become Career Record evidence automatically. Relevant evidence may be
offered for explicit user-controlled addition. Do not optimize primarily for vanity,
addictive engagement, fabricated achievement, or pay-to-win organic reach.

## 7. University product

University is a **Graduate Transition & Employability System**, not merely a dashboard.
Core system direction:

`Cohorts -> Graduate Outcomes -> Program Intelligence -> Employer Alignment -> Career Readiness -> Employer Engagement -> Institutional Reporting`

Institutional metrics require explicit population, numerator, denominator, coverage, period,
source, freshness, and methodology. Named-person access is not automatic.

## 8. University affiliation and distribution

Adopt:

`Self-Declared Affiliation -> Institutional Verification -> Cohort Linkage`

- University email is optional and never mandatory.
- Minimum semantic states: `Declared / Verified / Needs Review`.
- Self-declaration never silently upgrades to institutional verification.
- Verification establishes affiliation/cohort relationship, not access to the user's full
  private Career Record/CV.
- University is also a distribution channel: individual utility drives registration;
  registration/verification improves cohort coverage; employers gain richer early-career
  supply.

## 9. Government posture

Government is not a fourth actor. JID may serve government as regulatory/reference context,
official program/opportunity source, ecosystem partner, or institutional customer.

Potential future capabilities:

- Government Integrations
- Program Infrastructure
- Career Transition & Workforce Intelligence
- Program & Policy Evaluation

Government status never grants automatic personal-data access. JID complements Jadarat,
NLO, and other official systems rather than claiming to replace them. JID-derived market
intelligence is not official national statistics unless valid authority/evidence exists.

## 10. Hiring Evidence & Selection Workflow

JID owns structured selection workflow and evidence governance; JID does not become a
proprietary psychometric publisher by default.

JID may own role criteria/evidence definition, structured screening, work-sample workflow,
structured interviews, anchored scorecards, candidate evidence comparison, decision/audit,
and assessment orchestration.

Specialized language, coding, cognitive/aptitude, psychometric, proctoring, and video
infrastructure should normally be integrated/partnered after scientific, legal, privacy,
security, and Saudi/Arabic suitability review.

No universal JID Candidate Score, employability percentage, culture-fit score, or
automated emotion/personality inference. Assessment results are purpose-bound and do not
enter the public Career Record automatically.

## 11. Capital / execution model

Adopt **Capital-Light, Proof-Financed Network Build**.

- JID must not depend on VC for survival.
- Do not buy growth before repeat utility/retention evidence.
- Early acquisition is founder-led and partner-led.
- Employer opportunities and University distribution are the first scalable B2B2C channels
  to validate.
- Build network density before geography.
- Every major money/time/AI/engineering spend should buy user evidence, payer evidence,
  operating leverage, risk reduction, or defensibility.
- Fundraising is an accelerator for a working system, not a substitute for evidence.
- Do not claim a proven network effect, data moat, or monopoly today.

Potential long-term defensibility is cumulative trusted evidence, provenance, Saudi
semantic depth, source operations, workflow standards, institutional distribution, and
coverage-honest outcomes.

Large-scale Professional / Social Layer build is sequenced after the first employer
economic loop.

## 12. Geographic expansion

Adopt **Saudi-First, GCC-Ready, Country-by-Country, Customer-Pulled**.

Saudi Arabia is the home market and proving ground. Core contracts should avoid needless
Saudi lock-in, while country-specific rules live behind governed market adapters.

No second GCC country is selected today. A new market requires real demand/distribution
pull, legal/privacy readiness, authorized opportunity supply, operating capacity, and a
credible economics path. Cross-border opportunity coverage or an existing customer's GCC
need may precede a full country launch.

## 13. Trust & Rights Constitution

Adopt the compact **JID Trust & Rights Constitution** with nine durable domains:

1. human dignity and agency;
2. truth and traceability;
3. purpose limitation and data minimization;
4. user rights and control;
5. security and privacy by design;
6. fairness, accessibility, and non-discrimination;
7. human accountability for consequential decisions;
8. institutional and commercial integrity; and
9. accountability, reversibility, and stop authority.

Product models, actors, waves, pricing, vendors, and technical choices are not
constitutional and remain in dated operating doctrine.

## 14. Pricing & packaging architecture

Adopt **Free Core + Paid Intelligence & Workflow** architecture, without adopting exact
SAR prices or final package names yet.

- Individual Core remains free and genuinely useful.
- Advanced recurring Individual intelligence/workflow may become JID Plus after repeat
  utility is proven.
- Employer is the first principal payer: paid design partner -> hybrid subscription +
  included capacity + metered specialist usage.
- University: paid readiness/methodology -> annual transition/outcomes workspace + visible
  implementation/integration fees where needed.
- Government: contract-only.

Prohibited: personal-data sale, pay-to-win organic ranking, privacy paywall, fabricated
verification, job guarantees, dark pricing, or manipulative career anxiety.

Exact prices wait for willingness-to-pay, cost-to-serve, paid continuation/renewal,
procurement, support, and usage evidence.

## 15. Multi-agent delivery model

- ChatGPT / Nebras: strategy, founder decisions, orchestration, decomposition, synthesis,
  verification.
- Claude Code: research, OSS intelligence, architecture, algorithms, methodology.
- Codex: canonical backend/data/security/contracts/integrations/tests/technical closeout.
- Cursor: Product UI/UX, frontend, Arabic-first responsive/accessibility execution.
- GitHub: shared source of truth and evidence handoff.

No agent owns JID independently. Codex subagents are disabled by default unless a packet
explicitly authorizes them.

## 16. Mandatory reuse gate

Before substantial greenfield capability implementation, research credible existing
systems/repositories/standards/libraries/vendors and record one or a bounded combination of:

`ADOPT / FORK / INTEGRATE / EXTRACT PATTERN / BUY-PARTNER / BUILD / REJECT`

Public code, stars, or feature similarity are not enough; license, source rights, security,
maintenance, privacy, localization, integration cost, and exit risk must be reviewed.
