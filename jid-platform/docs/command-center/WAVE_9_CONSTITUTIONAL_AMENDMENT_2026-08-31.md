# Wave 9 amendment — governed professional participation

**Effective:** 2026-08-31 (Asia/Riyadh)  
**Authority:** Founder-approved Wave 9 closure packet

This is the single narrow amendment reconciling the historical absolute prohibition on
follower/feed/social mechanics with the later founder decision in the dated Wave Operating
Model and Wave 9 packet. No durable Trust & Rights article is weakened.

- **Old product text:** no follower graph, likes, comments, reshares, algorithmic feed, or
  social-network mechanics.
- **New text:** Articles 1–9 permit dated founder doctrine to authorize bounded professional
  participation. Wave 9 authorizes only voluntary Individual connection requests,
  accept/decline, disconnect/block, owner-removable professional updates, privacy controls,
  and a relationship-scoped chronological surface.
- **Reason/evidence:** founder directions dated 2026-08-27 and 2026-08-31 supersede the older
  product rule. JID remains Career Infrastructure, not a generic social network.
- **Rights/actors:** Individuals control participation, audiences, and removal. Business,
  University, anonymous, blocked, and unrelated actors receive no private network data.
  Employer discoverability remains a separate opt-in.
- **Privacy/security review:** database authorization precedes delivery; the layer reads no
  Career Operations, Abhathli, private Career Record, application, University-private, or
  employer-private evidence.
- **Founder approval/effective date:** founder-issued Wave 9 packet; 2026-08-31.

Engagement farming, popularity/engagement ranking, viral or opaque manipulation, pay-to-win
reach, vanity metrics, hidden sponsorship, contact-data sale, and private-data exposure remain
forbidden. An update never becomes Career Record evidence automatically.
