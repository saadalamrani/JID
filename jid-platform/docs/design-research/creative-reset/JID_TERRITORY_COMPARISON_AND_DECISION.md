# Three territories: comparison and Founder decision

Same product truth, permissions, and seven experiences. Three organizing systems; none selected.

| Fundamental choice | A — The Living Edition | B — The Passage | C — The Evidence Bench |
|---|---|---|---|
| Emotional character | Dignity and authorship | Possibility and movement | Capability and precision |
| Organizing unit | Chapter and annotated passage | Named threshold and focused plane | Evidence object and its action |
| Spatial signature | Asymmetric reading spread, sources in margin | Open aperture, expanding task plane, destination dock | Object index, evidence area, decision strip |
| Navigation | Masthead → contents → chapter | Context → destination → task | Workspace → object → evidence/action |
| Attention model | Read, understand, act | Orient, choose, move | Inspect, compare, decide |
| Density | Typeset continuity, precise tables | Spacious orientation, concentrated task | Medium/high working density, reading modes |
| Palette | Paper / aubergine / oxblood | Midnight / mist / cyan, atmospheric depth | Porcelain / near-black / vermilion / citron |
| Typography | Arabic editorial display + text; Latin serif/sans | Open geometric Arabic and Latin | Robust Arabic; compact Latin grotesk |
| Motion | Connect passage and source | Preserve origin when entering a task | Connect selection and evidence detail |
| Individual | Professional record worth expressing accurately | Next step grounded in the record | Personal truth to inspect and use |
| Employer | Annotated selection dossier | Evidence-gated human workflow | Criteria-to-evidence comparison bench |
| University | Coverage-honest institutional briefing | Observation plane with visible limits | Result beside measurement basis |
| Main danger | Publishing aesthetics outweigh evidence | Atmosphere implies predictive intelligence | ATS severity or people-as-inventory framing |
| Mobile | Margin becomes inline annotation | Panorama becomes ordered planes | Split bench becomes sequential review |

## Direct critique

**A:** Strong when reading is the work. It treats personal truth as seriously as an institutional report. Frequent employer comparison is its weak point: the spread must not slow inspection. Without marginal provenance and derived-expression relationships it could become a handsome publishing template.

**B:** A substantial spatial and emotional departure, but novelty is not utility. It can express movement without inventing a destination; it can also drift into generic AI/fintech atmosphere. It must remain understandable with gradients and animation removed. Unusual navigation needs an equally good conventional path.

**C:** Strong when evidence inspection is the work. It makes the employer proposition legible, but may overfit it. If an Individual feels processed, scored, or assigned tickets, it fails even if the hiring matrix looks excellent. Public and first-run experiences must remain human enough to teach the model.

These are qualitative critiques, not numerical ratings or a ranking. The same empty university state is deliberate: no territory gets a visual advantage from invented outcomes.

## Founder decision questions

1. Which organizing idea feels like JID's long-term identity: an edition, a passage, or a bench? Describe the idea before discussing color.
2. Which would you recognize after replacing its palette with grayscale?
3. Which helps an early-career Individual begin without work history, public popularity, or a polished CV?
4. Which employer screen most clearly shifts attention toward evidence and an accountable decision?
5. Which university screen makes uncertainty and privacy feel like honest operating conditions rather than missing dashboard content?
6. Which shell would you want to use repeatedly in Arabic on a phone? Name the navigation behavior that supports that preference.
7. Which risk would you fund further design work to resolve: editorial efficiency, spatial learnability, or operational warmth?
8. Is the reaction “this is attractive” or “this is how JID should work”? Identify a concrete interaction that supports the second answer.
9. Is any territory still too close to the rejected direction? Identify the structure, not only an adjective.
10. Choose one for deeper design, request a targeted revision, or reject all three. No selection is assumed.

## Review and decision record

Review Public Front Door, Individual no-data state, Career Record, Opportunity disclosure, Organization verification/review, Employer comparison, and University unavailable/suppressed state. Keep the same experience and language while switching territories. Compare Arabic and English, then narrow layouts. Do not select from the hero alone.

Record: **selected territory or none; thesis to preserve; experiences that convinced you; concrete concerns; next design scope**. A selected palette is not a selected experience. Do not blend all three into a compromise before judging each as a whole.

Later selection opens a bounded design-development packet: audition typefaces, deepen interaction/state design, test Arabic and accessibility, reconcile live capabilities. It is not implementation authorization. This packet stops before that work.
