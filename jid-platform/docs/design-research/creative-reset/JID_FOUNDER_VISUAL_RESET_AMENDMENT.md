## Anti-Slop after concept formation

First define a strong JID-specific thesis and give it a coherent visual form. Then critique the result:

1. What JID relationship, action, or emotional need does the technique clarify?
2. Would the same composition work unchanged for unrelated HR software? If so, what is missing?
3. Does the treatment imply unsupported intelligence, activity, verification, scale, or access?
4. Does it remain legible in Arabic and English, operable by keyboard and touch, and understandable without color or motion?
5. Can it meet performance and reduced-motion requirements without losing its meaning?

Gradients, glow, glass, expressive typography, unusual navigation, motion, atmospheric backgrounds, illustration, and cards are eligible techniques. Retain, revise, or remove them based on purpose and evidence after seeing the concept. Never use a ban list to collapse exploration before it begins. Trust and privacy prohibitions apply throughout, not only after concept review.
