# Territory A — The Living Edition | النسخة الحيّة

**A design proposition, not a selected direction or live product.** Governed by [Active Execution Authority](JID_ACTIVE_EXECUTION_AUTHORITY.md). The Arabic name is a working territory label, not a proposed replacement for جِد.

## Creative thesis

A career deserves the care of a substantial publication: facts composed clearly, evidence close at hand, and the person able to determine what is expressed. JID becomes a living professional edition. Its organizing unit is the **chapter**, its distinguishing gesture the **source in the margin**, and its emotional promise professional dignity without self-promotion.

This is not the old registrar metaphor in a cream palette. Composition is deliberately editorial and assertive: large Arabic titles, asymmetric openings, sectional folios, a clear reading cadence, and long evidence passages. The interface has no permanent dashboard rail. Content determines the page's shape.

## Character and Saudi relevance

Cultured, human, candid, and self-possessed. It belongs to JID because a single canonical record can support different purposeful expressions without becoming several conflicting biographies. Employer decisions and university reports receive the same care as a person's own account.

Saudi relevance comes from Arabic as the original composition, the movement between education and employment, and language that respects early-career work even when paid experience is absent. Avoid official seals, palm/sword motifs, faux heritage textures, borrowed media-brand styling, and exaggerated prestige. Do not imply that polished prose is stronger evidence.

## Identity, palette, typography

Visual identity: a confident جِد masthead, generous counters, a modest Latin companion, and a recurring marginal source rule. Explore a custom wordmark only after selection; no mark is adopted here.

Palette proposal: ink aubergine `#321D36`, soft paper `#F4EEE6`, oxblood `#842E45`, and pale apricot `#F1C9AC`. Aubergine carries reading; oxblood indicates an action or active section; apricot marks a selected passage without implying verification. This is a print-like tonal system, not olive/gold recoloring. Semantic warnings/errors will have separate accessible roles. No claim that these tokens alone constitute a validated accessible system.

Arabic direction: contemporary Naskh-derived display with strong horizontal rhythm, paired with an open, restrained Arabic text face for forms and dense evidence. Latin direction: a sturdy editorial serif for display and a quiet humanist sans for UI. Match apparent size and weight, not nominal point size; test Arabic diacritics and long qualification names. Typeface categories are a commissioning brief, not selected vendors or downloaded fonts. No forced tracking on Arabic.

## Composition, density, navigation, surfaces

Desktop uses an asymmetric page: approximately two-thirds reading area and one-third source/context margin. The start edge carries chapter identifiers; source notes occupy the far margin. A running masthead and a compact contents control provide global orientation. Object navigation is an in-page chapter index, not a permanent app sidebar. In Arabic the composition starts at the right; English is recomposed at the left with the same hierarchy.

Public pages are expansive, Individual pages medium density, and hiring/outcomes pages tightly typeset where comparison needs it. Whitespace separates changes in meaning, not every fact. Panels are paper inserts used for a discrete review or disclosure; ordinary entries are paragraphs and ruled sections. Tables look like annotated schedules: stable column headings, strong row alignment, source notes beside the relevant item. Avoid decorative page-curl effects or fake paper aging.

## Imagery, visualization, motion, mobile

Imagery direction: carefully commissioned documentary details of actual work, learning, tools, or artifacts with permission and accurate captions. Never stock portraits standing in for users or fake graduate testimonials. The initial system can be entirely typographic. Illustrations explain a source becoming an authorized expression without depicting a universal institutional database.

Real-data visualization would use directly labeled editorial charts with the method attached, no decorative sparklines. Tables remain the fallback. On mobile the margin becomes an inline disclosure immediately after the selected passage; it never floats over consent or submission. Chapter contents becomes a compact contents sheet, and reading order remains continuous.

Motion is a brief repositioning of a source note beside the selected fact, plus restrained section transitions. No simulated page flips, slow reveal of legal content, or motion required to understand provenance. Reduced motion uses instant state changes.

Accessibility implications: display type needs close scrutiny at zoom; the serif/sans pairing cannot make Arabic secondary; source notes must remain full-sized and in a meaningful reading order. Wide hiring tables need an equivalent candidate-by-criterion reading view on narrow screens, retaining the criterion label for every evidence item.

## Critique and trade-offs

The strength is dignity and legibility across personal and institutional truth. The risk is that it feels like publishing, or rewards eloquence over actual evidence. The safeguard is a clear separation between the record fact, optional narrative, source, and authorized expression. Dense hiring work may require a more compact table treatment; preserving the editorial thesis must not make comparison laborious.

Post-concept Anti-Slop verdict: the large type and paper color have purpose, but are insufficient differentiation by themselves. The territory succeeds only if the marginal provenance, chapter navigation, and derived expressions survive every operational screen. Remove any decorative quotation, empty prestige spread, or ornamental rule that obscures the next action.

Unmistakably JID: the person's professional account, employer evidence, and coverage-honest institutional briefing share one **fact-and-basis grammar**, while permissions keep their information separate. It does not organize itself around vacancies, applicants as inventory, a public popularity profile, or a grid of modules.

Founder stress test: does the no-data home still feel like a useful professional beginning, and does a long employer evidence comparison remain fast? Select this direction only if the publication-like reading model helps both.
