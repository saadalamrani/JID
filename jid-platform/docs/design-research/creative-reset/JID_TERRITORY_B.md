# Territory B — The Passage | العبور

**A design proposition, not a selected direction or live product.** Governed by [Active Execution Authority](JID_ACTIVE_EXECUTION_AUTHORITY.md). Working territory name only.

## Creative thesis

JID is the space between established professional truth and an action a person can take. Give that space a visible form. The organizing unit is a **threshold**: what is known here, what is needed to move, and what the human chooses to do. Its signature is an open central aperture with information arranged around its edges, rather than a page full of sections or a dashboard grid.

Movement is not a promised career trajectory. A line may connect a record to a review step only when that relationship exists. Uncertain futures are not plotted as predicted destinations. The atmosphere communicates possibility; operational surfaces communicate limits.

## Character and Saudi relevance

Expansive, contemporary, composed, and quietly ambitious. It belongs to JID because the platform connects education, professional evidence, opportunity, and selection without reducing them to vacancy browsing. It takes the transition itself seriously.

Saudi relevance is rooted in entry into professional life, movement between university and employer contexts, and Arabic spatial hierarchy. Avoid desert dunes, city skylines, futuristic megaproject imagery, flags, official emblems, and implied national endorsement. The concept should feel at home in Saudi life without requiring a tourism image or an imported science-fiction control room.

## Identity, palette, typography

Identity: a compact, decisive جِد wordmark placed at the origin of each surface. An open frame/aperture is the recurring motif, never a seal or verification badge. The frame marks the transition from context to an available action; it does not enclose the human as a data point.

Palette proposal: deep midnight `#102D43`, mist `#EAF5F4`, clear cyan `#75DDD2`, and warm coral `#FFBA9D`. An atmospheric midnight-to-blue-green gradient is eligible on public openings and quiet peripheral areas. Essential text and all forms sit on opaque surfaces with stable contrast. Cyan marks the active threshold, coral a human action or emphasis—not candidate quality or success. The sky-like space is intentionally open; it contains no fictional stars representing users or jobs.

Arabic direction: contemporary open geometric Arabic with generous bowls and strong distinction at small sizes; expressive breadth in short headings without elongating or tracking connected letters. Latin direction: a similarly open geometric grotesk, restrained weights and sentence case. Do not merely pair two faces called “geometric”; inspect proportions and long mixed-script labels. Exact font procurement remains a later decision.

## Composition, density, navigation, surfaces

The public opening is panoramic, with a central unfilled aperture and three spatially distinct actor entries. Workspaces use a compact destination dock at the bottom edge and one named context header; there is no permanent side rail. Selecting a task expands one focused work plane. The dock always uses readable labels and a conventional accessible menu alternative; no gesture-only navigation.

Density is elastic: spacious orientation, then opaque, concentrated task surfaces for evidence or forms. The visual field does not remain huge while the user is working. A surface represents an active task or boundary, not every datum. Tables occupy the work plane fully when needed; no tiny tables suspended in glass tiles. Arabic starts from the right; conceptual progression and arrows follow language, while the factual chronology remains explicitly dated.

## Imagery, visualization, motion, mobile

Illustration is abstract spatial framing, not fabricated institutional infrastructure. A soft aperture, light transition, or carefully cropped real work image can signal a change in context. The system must still work with atmosphere removed. Decorative gradients never encode outcome, quality, permissions, or progress.

Data visualization stays on opaque planes with direct labels, source, period, and accessible table alternatives. Do not turn aggregate outcomes into flowing particles or Sankey-like streams without an appropriate real dataset and methodology.

Motion can show the selected work plane expanding from its labeled destination, preserving orientation. It must not delay form access or imply completed workflow. Reduced motion swaps immediately. No parallax needed for navigation, perpetual drift, blurred body text, or GPU-heavy background canvas.

On mobile the panorama becomes one vertical plane; the origin/context is at the top and labeled destinations stay reachable through a small dock/menu. A spatial relationship becomes an explicit ordered explanation. All evidence and disclosure review remain available before the primary action, which must not obscure content or the keyboard.

Accessibility implications: a spatial metaphor is the highest risk of the three territories. Reading and focus order must remain meaningful without position; an ordinary list/menu is a first-class navigation form. Test focus through expanding planes, large text, reduced motion, color contrast at gradient edges, and long Arabic errors. Glass, if explored, is limited to nonessential chrome and has an opaque fallback.

## Critique and trade-offs

The strength is a memorable sense of professional possibility that does not depend on prestige or popularity. The risk is that atmosphere overwhelms utility, resembles a fintech/AI landing page, or invents a smooth career journey that the product cannot promise. Use actual named relationships, stop conditions, and opaque task planes to keep it honest.

Post-concept Anti-Slop verdict: the gradient and aperture are allowed and purposeful, but become generic immediately if detached from record → action and evidence → decision. Reject animated fake nodes, decorative “intelligence” halos, and progress-like strokes without factual semantics. Density must expand during work; retain the territory only if the employer comparison and university missing-data state remain convincing without motion.

Unmistakably JID: the central experience is **crossing an authorized professional threshold with evidence**, across all three actors. The interface is not organized around an endless listing, social feed, pipeline inventory, or executive metrics dashboard.

Founder stress test: switch off animation and atmosphere. Does the spatial organization still explain the next action better than a conventional page? If not, the direction needs redesign rather than added effects.
