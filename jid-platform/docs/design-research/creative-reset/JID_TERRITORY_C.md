# Territory C — The Evidence Bench | طاولة الإثبات

**A design proposition, not a selected direction or live product.** Governed by [Active Execution Authority](JID_ACTIVE_EXECUTION_AUTHORITY.md). Working territory name only.

## Creative thesis

Professional trust is made through inspectable work. JID is a precise working bench on which a person, employer, or university can place a fact beside its basis and decide what to do. Its organizing unit is the **evidence object**. Its signature is a visible seam between **what is stated, what supports it, and the authorized action**.

This is an expressive instrument, not generic enterprise software: strong typography, sharp geometry, exposed relationships, saturated signal color, and a dense, deliberately useful work area. It earns distinctiveness through how people inspect evidence, not through a futuristic dashboard skin.

## Character and Saudi relevance

Capable, direct, exact, and energetic. It belongs to JID's Hiring Evidence & Selection Workflow while also giving an Individual the means to represent real work without performative posting. For universities it makes the basis and limits of an outcome as important as the result.

Saudi relevance comes through Arabic-first working language, the legibility of academic and professional evidence, and respect for practical accomplishment during the education-to-work transition. Avoid factory metaphors that make people inventory, government-service imitation, decorative Arabic calligraphy, and imported recruitment jargon. The bench serves the person; it does not evaluate their worth.

## Identity, palette, typography

Identity: a tightly composed جِد masthead, sharp corner joins, visible alignment rails, and a repeated three-part notation: statement / basis / action. Explore the mark's geometry later; avoid checkmarks or seals that imply certification.

Palette proposal: porcelain `#F4F5F0`, near-black `#16201E`, saturated vermilion `#BE3C22`, and electric citron `#DCEA73`. Near-black handles dense reading; vermilion is the action accent; citron identifies the actively inspected object without implying approval. Errors and destructive actions need distinct labels and semantic treatment so brand red does not become the only signal. This is a light working field with strong bounded areas, not a dark SaaS console.

Arabic direction: a robust contemporary sans with clear terminals, open counters, and distinguishable forms at operational sizes; weight supplies hierarchy, never narrow compression of connected letters. Latin direction: a compact grotesk for headings with tabular numerals for real dates and aligned quantities. Monospace, if used, is reserved for actual identifiers; never style Arabic prose as code. Specific font families remain subject to testing and licensing after selection.

## Composition, density, navigation, surfaces

The desktop work surface is a split bench: a compact object index at the reading start, a primary evidence area, and a bounded action/review strip at the far end. Global orientation is an object breadcrumb plus a labeled workspace menu, not a fixed list of modules. Changing the selected object updates the evidence area while retaining context. Forms can occupy the entire bench when split viewing is unnecessary.

Density is medium by default and high for comparison; Public remains bold and sparse enough to explain the product. Heavy rules mark different authority or action regions; lighter rules group ordinary data. Cards are permitted as bounded evidence objects and may differ in height because their source content differs. Tables carry real criterion comparisons, not dozens of ungrounded dashboard columns. No gratuitous terminal UI, window controls, serial numbers, or grid wallpaper.

## Imagery, visualization, motion, mobile

Imagery favors permissioned work artifacts and precise explanatory drawings: a project excerpt, a qualification's field structure, or the relationship between requirement and evidence. These are used only when available and authorized. Do not invent certificates, assessment results, uploaded documents, candidate faces, or institutional records for decoration.

Real-data visualizations use direct labels and a visible basis panel. A table is often sufficient. Empty analytical space contains an explanation, not a decorative chart or redacted fake values. Citron selection never means “better.”

Motion explains inspection: a selected object receives focus and its detail appears in place. No dragging needed for a consequential action, no candidate cards tossed across columns, no kinetic numbers. Keyboard selection and reduced motion provide the same result.

Mobile becomes object index → full-screen evidence detail → review/action. Back restores context and position. A comparison becomes sequential criterion-labeled evidence, with a summary before decision, not a microscopic squeezed matrix. Do not require desktop for the Individual's core application or privacy rights. Dense institutional work may be more efficient on desktop but remains readable and actionable where evidence is sufficient.

Accessibility implications: strong rules must not create a maze; reading order must match the objects and their labels. Selection, source state, and destructive actions need words and focus states as well as color. Review zoom, Arabic line wrapping, keyboard movement across tables, and touch target separation. Avoid spreadsheet interaction semantics unless they are actually implemented and tested in a future build.

## Critique and trade-offs

The strength is operational confidence and source inspection. The risk is a severe workplace tool that overwhelms a new graduate, feels like an ATS, or makes every relationship transactional. The Individual experience needs a calm first step, readable explanations, and ownership language. The public front door must explain all three actors before exposing dense work.

Post-concept Anti-Slop verdict: strong rules, sharp cards, and signal color earn their place by distinguishing evidence, context, and action. They become styling when every surface uses the same boxed matrix. Preserve softer reading modes for long text and onboarding; remove fake technical identifiers and pseudo-precision.

Unmistakably JID: a person owns their professional truth; an employer inspects purpose-bound evidence; a university inspects privacy-safe outcomes and their basis. The same **statement / basis / authorized action** structure serves all three without universal scoring, public popularity, or pipeline inventory as the primary metaphor.

Founder stress test: can a first-time Individual with no work history feel capable here, while an employer can inspect a difficult evidence comparison efficiently? The direction must pass both, not just the hiring screen.
