# R1-B Source Ledger

Companion to `R1B_JID_SAUDI_CONTENT_LANGUAGE_RESEARCH.md` and
`R1B_JID_SAUDI_PRODUCT_LANGUAGE_SYSTEM.md`. Follows R1-A's amended
evidence-confidence discipline: every source is classified
**PRIMARY/HIGH-CONFIDENCE** or **DIRECTIONAL/SECONDARY-SOURCE**, and any
DIRECTIONAL figure or claim below must not be promoted into JID marketing
copy, a product metric, an investor claim, a stated Saudi-market fact, a
KPI, or any other public statistic without independent, stronger
verification first (per `R1A_SOURCE_LEDGER.md`'s standing restriction,
carried forward here).

Two access constraints shaped this ledger: `thmanyah.com` and `uxbert.com`
were both blocked by this session's network egress proxy, so both
references are built from third-party coverage and search-result summaries
rather than a direct first-party read — each is marked accordingly below,
and no long passage from either site is reproduced anywhere in this
research (per the brief's copyright/research discipline).

## Notes on sourcing discipline (R1-B specific)

- No long passage from Thmanyah's or any other company's copy was reproduced. Where this research names a real JID copy example (J1/J2) it is quoted briefly because it is JID's own product text, not a third party's.
- Two intended primary sources (`thmanyah.com`, `uxbert.com`) were blocked by this session's network egress policy. Both references are therefore built from secondary coverage and are classified DIRECTIONAL/SECONDARY-SOURCE even though the underlying organizations are credible first-party Saudi references — a future pass with direct access to those sites could upgrade this classification.
- Per the brief's stop condition, several categories (mobility, additional creative agencies) were scanned briefly and not pursued once they produced no new language principle beyond what the retained sources already established; they are not listed here to avoid a padded bibliography.
- **Downstream use restriction (carried from R1-A):** no figure or claim sourced from a DIRECTIONAL/SECONDARY-SOURCE entry above may be promoted into JID marketing copy, a product metric, an investor claim, a stated Saudi-market fact, a KPI, or any other public statistic without independent, stronger verification first.
